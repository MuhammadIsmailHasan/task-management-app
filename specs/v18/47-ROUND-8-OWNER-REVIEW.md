# Round 8 Owner Review, V18
### Requirement 73: Round 8 Owner Review
**User Story:** As the owner, I want the fixes and small features from the round 8 review recorded, so that the specs match the build.

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. [R8-01] EVERY HTML email (confirm email, confirm new email, password changed) SHALL use one layout: a brand header, a white card, a heading, the greeting with the user's name HTML-escaped, a "Confirm email" button and the full link as text when there is a link, and a muted note; colours and sizes come from the Design_System values. THE plain-text part SHALL stay.
2. [R8-02] WHEN `MAIL_BACKEND=smtp`, THE Backend SHALL refuse to start unless `PUBLIC_BASE_URL` starts with `http://` or `https://`, because every link in a mail starts with it.
3. [R8-03] (Replaces the default destination of TOK-03.) WHEN login succeeds without a Safe_Return_Path in `next`, THE Frontend SHALL navigate a Supervisor, a Manager, and an Admin to `/app/dashboard` and a Member to `/app/boards`. A Safe_Return_Path in `next` SHALL still win for every role.
4. [R8-04] `GET /api/users` items (Admin payloads only) SHALL include `email_verified` (boolean, `email_verified_at` is set). THE Users table SHALL show under each email a "Confirmed" (`accent`) or "Not confirmed" (`orange`) badge.
5. [R8-05] THE show and hide password button SHALL be centred by a flex wrapper, not a transform, so the 1 pixel press offset of every button does not move it off the field and a click anywhere on it toggles the password, with or without an error shown.
6. [R8-06] (Amends DASH-06.) THE Dashboard "Recent activity" card SHALL have no "View all" pill.
7. [R8-07] THE Dashboard card rows (Overdue, Due on, Top 5) and the rows of Recent activity and of the Side_Panel Activity view SHALL each be one link (`LinkRow`, the Card tile hover shadow) that opens the Card over its Board. A row about a deleted Card or about a Board, Column, or user SHALL stay plain text. Opening a Card from the Side_Panel SHALL close the panel.
8. [R8-08] THE `RoleTag` SHALL cover the four roles (Member `neutral`, Supervisor `blue`, Manager `purple`, Admin `amber`). THE Dashboard greeting, the My tasks title (also when empty), and the Boards title SHALL each be followed by a `RoleTag` reading "as <Role>".
9. [R8-09] (Amends TOAST-05.) A failure Toast SHALL use the `warning` tone (`--warning-soft` and `--on-warning-soft`, set from the `amber` label pair) for HTTP 403, 409, and 429, and the `danger` tone (`--danger-soft` and `--on-danger-soft`) for every other failure; success and info Toasts stay neutral. Toasts with the same message SHALL be one Toast (the message is its id).
10. [R8-10] THE Board view SHALL show no inline error alert: a failed Card or Column action is a Toast (R8-09) only. A link to a deleted Card SHALL show the `warning` Toast "This card is no longer available.", a link to an archived Card "This card is archived.", and both SHALL remove `?card=` from the address.
11. [R8-11] AN activity line SHALL wrap any long word (for example an attachment link used as its name) inside its row (`min-w-0`, `wrap-anywhere`).
12. [R8-12] THE Calendar view SHALL mark today with an `--accent` border on its cell and its day number on `--accent` with `--on-accent`; every other cell keeps a transparent border of the same width.
13. [R8-13] `flask seed-data` SHALL add 6 Boards (Website Redesign, Mobile App Launch, Customer Support Desk, Marketing Campaign Q1, Platform Migration, Hiring and Q1 Planning) with people's names, Cards in every Column, subtasks, comments, and due dates around the seed day. `SEED_ADMIN_PASSWORD` SHALL give the Admin its own password. `deploy/bare/seed.sh` SHALL ask for both passwords (different, at least 8 characters) and, with `--reset`, SHALL ask the operator to type `erase`, write a backup to `/var/backups/revoboard`, stop the app, recreate and migrate the database, seed, and start the app.
14. [R8-14] THE repository SHALL drop `design/` (the prototypes are built) and the Docker server scripts (`deploy/deploy.sh`, `deploy/seed.sh`, `deploy/setup-server.sh`, `deploy/supervisor/`). CI SHALL always deploy with `deploy/bare/deploy.sh`; Docker stays for the CI smoke test only.
