# Feedback and Design Consistency, V18 (draft)
### Requirement 60: Toasts
**User Story:** As a user, I want every action to answer me in one place, so that I always know whether it worked without scanning the page.
This requirement adds a Toast channel (Decision BK). It supersedes the inline success messages and the non-field error banners of earlier versions for **action results**. It does not change the page states (loading, error with Retry, empty), the field errors of forms, the V12 auto-save status, or the error area of an open ConfirmDialog (Open Question 80).
#### Acceptance Criteria
1. [TOAST-01] THE Frontend SHALL show a Toast at the bottom right of the viewport for the result of every user action that writes data (create, update, delete, move, invite, join, restore, upload, mark read in bulk, sign out excepted): a success Toast after a 2xx answer and an error Toast after a non-2xx answer or a network failure. THE Toast library SHALL be `sonner`, wrapped once in `components/shared/BaseToaster.tsx` and called only through `lib/toast.ts` (`toast.success(message)`, `toast.error(message)`), so no component imports `sonner` directly.
2. [TOAST-02] THE error Toast SHALL show the API Envelope `message` (or "Something went wrong. Try again." for a network failure or an empty message); IT SHALL NOT show a stack, a code, or a raw status. THE success Toast SHALL use a short past-tense sentence naming the thing ("Column added", "Card restored to To Do", "User updated").
3. [TOAST-03] WHILE the viewport is below 960 pixels, THE Toasts SHALL appear at the bottom centre, full width minus the page gutter, above the mobile safe area. AT most 3 Toasts SHALL be visible; a fourth pushes out the oldest.
4. [TOAST-04] A success Toast SHALL close after 4 seconds and an error Toast after 6 seconds; both SHALL pause while hovered or focused and SHALL have a close control with the name "Dismiss". THE Toaster region SHALL be an `aria-live` region (`polite` for success, `assertive` for error) so a screen reader announces it, and a Toast SHALL NOT take focus.
5. [TOAST-05] THE Toast SHALL use Design_System tokens only: `--surface-raised`, `--ink`, `--border`, the `success` and `danger` tones for the icon, `--radius-md`, `--shadow-lg`, the spacing scale of Requirement 61, and the light and dark themes. (Amended by Requirement 73: R8-09 adds the `warning` and `danger` failure tones.)
6. [TOAST-06] A field error of a form (a 400 `VALIDATION_ERROR` with `details`) SHALL stay next to its field (Requirement 45) and SHALL NOT also raise a Toast; a 400 without `details` raises an error Toast. WHILE a ConfirmDialog is open, its action error SHALL stay in the dialog (COL-05) and SHALL NOT raise a Toast; a success closes the dialog and raises a success Toast.
7. [TOAST-07] Auto-saved edits (the Card modal fields of Requirement 25) SHALL keep the inline "Saving / Saved" status and SHALL raise only error Toasts, not a success Toast per keystroke save.

### Requirement 61: Design Consistency
**User Story:** As the product owner, I want the same space, size, and colour rules on every screen, so that the product looks like one product.
This requirement adds scales to the Design_System (Decision BL) and applies them to every screen of V1 to V18. It supersedes any per-component spacing written before V18 that conflicts with it.
#### Acceptance Criteria
**Scales**
1. [DS-01] THE Design_System SHALL define these tokens in `styles/tokens.css` and map them in `@theme` so Tailwind utilities use them: `--space-label` 8px (label to control), `--space-field` 16px (between form fields and between controls in a toolbar), `--space-section` 24px (between sections and between cards of a grid), `--space-page` 24px below 960 pixels and 32px from 960 pixels (page gutter and the space under the page title), `--control-h` 40px (input, select, button, search, date field), `--control-h-sm` 32px (compact controls in a Column header and table rows), `--chip-h` 24px (badge, pill, chip, filter chip), and `--progress-h` 8px (progress bars).
2. [DS-02] EVERY input, select, combobox, search field, date field, and button of default size SHALL be `--control-h` high, and every compact control `--control-h-sm`; the text size inside follows the existing type scale (`text-sm`). No component SHALL set its own height with a number.
3. [DS-03] (Text size amended by DS-22, round 4: chips use `sm`.) EVERY chip-like element (priority badge, due badge, category pill, column badge, role badge, filter chip, count pill, "Overdue" date chip) SHALL be `--chip-h` high with the same horizontal padding, radius (`--radius-full`), and text size (`text-xs`, `font-medium`). THE Board_Toolbar progress pill and its bar SHALL use `--chip-h` and `--progress-h`; the toolbar search field SHALL use `--control-h`, so the toolbar has two heights only.
4. [DS-04] EVERY labelled field SHALL put `--space-label` between the label and the control, and a hint or error line SHALL sit `--space-label` below the control. Forms SHALL stack fields with `--space-field`, and form sections with `--space-section`.
5. [DS-05] EVERY page SHALL use `--space-page` as its gutter and below its title block, and SHALL separate its top-level blocks (filters, widgets, tables) with `--space-section`.
6. [DS-06] A grep in CI (`frontend/scripts/check-tokens.mjs`, already used for colours) SHALL also fail on an arbitrary spacing, height, or width value (`p-[`, `gap-[`, `h-[`, `w-[`, `m-[` with a number) outside `components/ui/`.
**Lists and options**
7. [DS-07] A list of options (`BaseCombobox`, `BaseSelect`, `BaseMenu`, the command menu) SHALL NOT show any option as highlighted when it opens. AN option SHALL be highlighted only while the pointer is over it or after the first arrow key, and the highlight SHALL clear when the pointer leaves the list. THE chosen value SHALL be marked with a check icon and `aria-selected="true"`, never with the highlight colour alone. This supersedes the cmdk default (first item selected on open) everywhere.
8. [DS-08] EVERY select in the product SHALL be a `BaseCombobox` that can be searched by typing, with the same trigger look (`--surface`, `--border`, `--control-h`, chevron), the same popover (`--surface-raised`, `--shadow-lg`), and the same empty line ("No matches"). A plain native `<select>` SHALL NOT be used.
**Date and check controls**
9. [DS-09] THE Frontend SHALL have one date field, `BaseDateField` (the field already used for the Card modal dates, over `react-day-picker`), and every date input (Card modal start and end, Dashboard range and "Due on", any other) SHALL use it: a trigger that shows the date in the user's locale format, a calendar popover, a Clear control where the date is optional, and the keyboard rules of `react-day-picker`. A native `<input type="date">` SHALL NOT be used.
10. [DS-10] EVERY checkbox SHALL be `BaseCheckbox` (one size, `--radius-sm`, the accent tone when checked, a visible focus ring); EVERY on/off setting SHALL be `BaseSwitch`. THE Card modal's "Start date" and "End date" toggles SHALL use `BaseCheckbox` with a label, aligned to the date field it enables.
**Surfaces**
11. [DS-11] A form control SHALL be visually distinct from the surface it sits on: on `--canvas` a control uses `--surface`, on `--surface` it uses `--surface-sunken`, and every control has a `--border` that meets 3:1 against its background in both themes.
12. [DS-12] A focus ring SHALL never be clipped: a scroll container that holds focusable controls SHALL have inner padding at least `--border-width-focus` plus the ring offset, or the control SHALL use an inset ring.
**Verification**
13. [DS-13] THE owner's visual review (Requirement 72) SHALL check every screen against DS-01 to DS-32 at 360, 414, 960, and 1280 pixels.
**Shape and control consistency (round 2 within V18)**
14. [DS-14] (Superseded by DS-18, round 3 within V18.) THE Design_System SHALL give each radius token one role, and every component SHALL use the token of its role: `--radius-field` for every form control (input, search field, textarea, `BaseCombobox` trigger, `BaseDateField` trigger, colour swatch trigger, the search field inside a list, and the option rows of a list); `--radius-full` for buttons, icon buttons, chips, badges, pills, the segmented control, switches, avatars, dots, and progress bars; `--radius-card` for items inside a surface (Card tiles, list and table rows, widget rows, comment and attachment tiles); `--radius-frame` for surfaces (page frame, Sidebar, sections, Columns, filter card, popovers, modals, the Side_Panel); `--radius-sm` for the checkbox only. A search field and a select placed side by side SHALL therefore have the same radius.
15. [DS-15] EVERY form control SHALL share one look from one shared class (`controlClasses` in `components/ui/input.tsx`): `--control-h` height (`--control-h-sm` for the compact size), `--radius-field`, `--surface` fill, the same border, padding, text size, placeholder colour, hover, focus ring, disabled, and invalid states. `BaseInput`, `BaseTextarea` (taller, same radius), the `BaseCombobox` trigger, the `BaseDateField` trigger, and the swatch trigger SHALL use it; a select SHALL NOT be drawn as a button pill.
16. [DS-16] NO component SHALL use a magic number: no arbitrary Tailwind value (`[...]` with a length, colour, radius, shadow, or duration), no inline `style` with a literal length or colour (a computed percentage such as a progress width is allowed), and no raw hex, `rgb`, or `px` value outside `styles/tokens.css`. Every size, space, radius, colour, shadow, z-index, duration, and opacity SHALL come from a named token.
17. [DS-17] A test (`frontend/tests/design-system.test.ts`) SHALL fail the build WHEN a file outside `components/ui/` and `styles/` breaks DS-16, or WHEN a form control listed in DS-15 uses a radius other than `--radius-field`, or WHEN a radius utility is used outside its DS-14 role in the shared components. This test replaces the `check-tokens` script of DS-06.
**One radius and two paddings (round 3 within V18; supersedes DS-14)**
18. [DS-18] THE Design_System SHALL have exactly two radius tokens: `--radius-ui` (12 pixels), used by every component (buttons, icon buttons, chips, badges, inputs, selects, date fields, tabs, the segmented control, progress pills and bars, Card tiles, rows, Columns, sections, the Sidebar, popovers, menus, tooltips, toasts, and modals), and `--radius-full`, used only for circles (avatars, status dots, and the switch). No other radius token or utility SHALL exist. A search field, a progress pill, a select, and a Card SHALL therefore have the same corners.
19. [DS-19] THE Design_System SHALL pad blocks with two tokens: `--pad-surface` (24 pixels; 32 pixels from 960 pixels) for page-level surfaces (sections, widget and filter cards, profile cards, modals and their header and footer), and `--pad-item` (16 pixels; 20 pixels from 960 pixels) for items and dense surfaces (Card tiles, rows, Columns, the Board toolbar, the Sidebar, popovers). THE page gutter `--page-x` SHALL be 24 pixels, and 40 pixels from 960 pixels; the gap between Columns and between Cards `--gutter` SHALL be 16 pixels, and 24 pixels from 960 pixels. A component SHALL NOT pad a surface or an item with a raw spacing step.
20. [DS-20] EVERY form control SHALL draw its focus ring inside its own edge (`focus-ring-inset`), so no scroll container can clip it, and every scroll container that holds other focusable elements (the Card modal panes) SHALL have inner padding of at least the ring width plus its offset; the activity timeline dots SHALL sit inside their list, never cut by the pane edge.
**Board layout and one type scale (round 4 within V18)**
21. [DS-21] THE Board's Column row SHALL put its padding on an inner row, so the last Column keeps the same `--page-x` gutter as the first while the row scrolls sideways; every Column, the Add column tile, and their contents (header, cards, "No cards", Add Card) SHALL share the one `--pad-item` padding with no extra inset. BELOW 960 pixels a Column is `--column-w` wide; FROM 960 pixels the Columns share the row equally and never get narrower than `--column-w-min` (16rem); more Columns scroll. THE Column name SHALL be `text-base`, semibold, and truncate. THE App_Header SHALL show the brand (not the Board name) below 960 pixels, because the Board name is the page title.
22. [DS-22] THE Design_System SHALL have seven text sizes: `xs` (12, labels, badges, eyebrows), `sm` (14, body, controls, chips, toolbar), `base` (15, Card titles), `page-title` (18), `title` (20), `xl` (24), and `display` (28; 34 from 960). `text-2xs`, `text-meta`, and the `text-card-*` sizes are removed. Every control and chip in one toolbar SHALL use the same size (`sm`): the Board filter chips, the progress pill, and the search field.
23. [DS-23] THE Board header SHALL NOT show the line "N Columns · N Total Tasks · description" (round 4).
**Balanced type, auth and landing (round 5 within V18)**
24. [DS-24] EVERY heading SHALL take its size from its level, and nothing else: `h1` is `xl` (the landing hero alone is `display`), `h2` is `page-title`, and `h3` and `h4` are `base` semibold (an `xs` uppercase eyebrow is allowed for a group label). The modal and dialog title is `page-title`; every section inside the Card modal (Details, Description, Attachments, Subtasks, Comments, Activity) is the same `h3`. Body text, controls, and chips are `sm`; labels, badges, and meta lines are `xs`. THE `title` size (20 pixels) is removed, so the scale is six sizes: `xs`, `sm`, `base`, `page-title`, `xl`, `display` (this amends DS-22). No new token is added.
25. [DS-25] EVERY app page SHALL use one page frame: `px-page`, `pt-surface`, `pb-surface`, and `gap-section` between its blocks; this includes the Archived, List, Calendar, Dashboard, My Tasks, Users, Boards, and Profile pages. THE Archived page SHALL drop its "Back to board" button (the back arrow and the Top_Menu lead back) and report Restore with a Toast ("Card restored to <Column>").
26. [DS-26] THE sign-in and register pages SHALL use the same scale: the one radius, `p-surface`, the brand panel heading as `h2` `page-title` with one short line, the logo, the Sign in or Create account switch (`--control-h-sm` items), the `h1` title, and the form sharing one left edge, `gap-field` between fields, and the default (accent) button for the submit.
27. [DS-27] THE landing page SHALL use the same scale and carry little text: the hero (one `display` line, one `sm` line, Get started and Sign in), Features (four items, a title and one short line each), Workflow (the five stage names), Roles (the five role names and one line), one closing call to action, and a one-row footer. THE Teams and FAQ sections and the announcement bar are removed. No paragraph is longer than 16 words. Below 960 pixels the header shows the logo mark, so the logo and the call to action never overlap. (Workflow and Roles amended by Requirement 73: R8-17.)
**Column width, public board, rich text (round 6 within V18)**
28. [DS-28] THE Column width `--column-w` SHALL be a share of the board row, which is a size container: below 960 pixels one Column and a peek of the next; from 960 pixels three Columns and half of the fourth (`(100cqw - 2 x --page-x - 3 x --gutter) / 3.5`). Fewer Columns grow to fill the row; the row keeps `--page-x` on both ends while it scrolls (amends DS-21; `--column-w-min` is removed, no token is added). THE public board (Shared_View) SHALL use the same frame, header line, eyebrow and `h1`, filter toolbar, Column row, Column header, and Card tiles as the Board, with a "Read only" badge and a Sign in or Go to Home button in its header; no token is added for it.
29. [DS-29] THE Board description and the Card description SHALL be rich text edited with TinyMCE, self-hosted from `/tinymce` (copied from `node_modules` by `scripts/copy-tinymce.mjs` before `dev` and `build`; no cloud key; the GPL licence key). THE editor SHALL offer only: paragraph, heading, subheading, bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, bulleted and numbered lists, link, quote, and clear formatting; its content area SHALL be styled from the design tokens at runtime and its frame SHALL use the one radius, the border token, and the inset focus ring. THE Backend SHALL sanitise every description with one allowlist (`app/common/rich_text.py`, `nh3`: `p br strong em u s ul ol li a h2 h3 blockquote code pre`, `href` and `title` on links, `http`, `https`, `mailto` only, `rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow"`), store `null` for an empty editor, limit a Board description to 500 visible characters, and a Card description to 20000 characters of HTML. THE Frontend SHALL sanitise again before display (DOMPurify, same allowlist) and SHALL show plain words (no tags) in Card tiles, Board tiles, and search. THE migration `d4a7e1c9b215` SHALL make `boards.description` TEXT and turn every existing plain-text description into one escaped paragraph with its line breaks. The Card description saves on blur (Requirement 25).
**Editor inside the Card modal, option rows, board edge (round 7 within V18)**
30. [DS-30] THE TinyMCE dialogs and menus (rendered outside every modal) SHALL work inside the Card modal: a click, a focus, or an Escape in them SHALL NOT close the Card modal, the modal's focus trap SHALL NOT pull focus out of their fields, and Cancel, the close button, Escape, and Save SHALL close only the TinyMCE dialog. THEIR look SHALL follow the system (the one radius, the app fonts, `page-title` for the dialog title, the accent primary button). THE editor frame SHALL show one focus ring (the ring colour on the border token width), and TinyMCE's own inner edit-area border SHALL be off.
31. [DS-31] EVERY option row of a list (`BaseCombobox`, command menu) SHALL be at least `--control-h` high with `px-3`, `py-2`, and `gap-3`, so a rich row (avatar, name, email, role tag) has room; the assignee list SHALL use the wide popover and the `md` avatar.
32. [DS-32] THE board's Column row SHALL be inset by `--page-x` on both sides as a scroll area, so the Columns are clipped at the same right edge as the toolbar above them; the Column width SHALL be computed from that inset area (`(100cqw - 3 x --gutter) / 3.5` from 960 pixels), and the public board SHALL do the same (amends DS-28).
