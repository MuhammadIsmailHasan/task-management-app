# App Shell, V16 (draft)

### Requirement 39: App Shell

**User Story:** As a signed-in user, I want the prototype's icon rail, pill navigation, and board dropdown, so that I can reach every screen and switch Boards from a familiar, balanced layout.

The prototype draws both an icon rail and a top pill nav, and places a logo in each. V16 keeps both because the owner chose the prototype shell (Open Question 39), but gives the logo one place, the App_Header, so the brand is not shown twice. The shell looks as the references in `design/reference/v2/` show it (canonical pages 1, 10, 11, 12, and 13) and is sized by Requirement 38. This requirement replaces the Sidebar (Decision AT). Behaviour of the Inbox, Activity, Categories, members, invites, and public link is unchanged; only where it is reached and how it looks changes.

#### Acceptance Criteria

**Navigation**

1. [SHELL-01] WHILE the viewport is 960 pixels or wider, THE Frontend SHALL show the Icon_Rail on every Protected_Route page as the prototype draws it: a white pill, `--rail-w` wide, floating at the left of the canvas with a `--page-x` margin above, below, and at its left, and a `--space-4` gap to the app frame (reference `01-board-desktop.png`). It holds, from the top: the Home circle (`--accent`, 44 pixels, white icon, links to `/boards`, accessible name "Home"), then the items Board (only while a Board is open; links to that Board), Dashboard, Inbox, Activity, Categories, and Team, in this order and with nothing at the bottom. Which items appear for which role is criterion 5.
2. [SHELL-02] THE Frontend SHALL draw each Icon_Rail item as a 40 pixel circle holding a `lucide-react` icon in `--ink-muted`, with an accessible name equal to its label and a `BaseTooltip` showing the label on hover and on keyboard focus only; WHEN the pointer leaves the item or it loses focus, THE Frontend SHALL hide the tooltip, and a click SHALL NOT leave it showing. THE active item SHALL be a filled `--accent` circle with a `--on-accent` icon (Requirement 38 criterion 18, Rail item) and, for a route item, `aria-current="page"`; the Inbox, Activity, and Team items are active while the Side_Panel shows their view.
3. [SHELL-03] WHILE the viewport is 960 pixels or wider, THE Frontend SHALL show the Top_Nav in the App_Header, centred between the logo and the bell, with the items Boards, Dashboard, Inbox, Activity, Categories, and Team as text labels, drawn as the pill track of Requirement 38 criterion 18 (reference `01-board-desktop.png`): the active item is a `--surface-sunken` pill with semibold text; the Inbox item shows the unread count as a teal count badge while the count is above zero, capped at "9+".
4. [SHELL-04] WHILE the viewport is below 960 pixels, THE Frontend SHALL hide the Icon_Rail and the Top_Nav and show in the persistent App_Header a menu button (44 pixels, accessible name "Open menu", `aria-expanded`) that opens a Nav_Drawer: an off-canvas panel from the left behind a scrim, built as `BaseDrawer` (a new shared wrapper over the shadcn/ui `Dialog`, added to the list of Requirement 14 criterion 10), listing the same items as 44 pixel rows with icon and label, then the Board_Switcher list of criterion 13. THE Nav_Drawer SHALL close on a route change, on a scrim click, and on Escape, trap focus while open, and return focus to the menu button.
5. [SHELL-05] THE Frontend SHALL show each item only to the roles that may use it: Boards (Top_Nav and drawer) and the Home circle (rail) to every signed-in user, and the rail's Board item to every user while a Board is open; Dashboard to a Supervisor and a Manager (Requirement 10); Inbox and Activity to every signed-in user; Categories to a Supervisor, a Manager, and an Admin, and only while a Board is open (Requirement 6 criterion 11); Team to every signed-in user while a Board is open, and to an Admin always (criterion 21). An item that is not shown takes no space.
6. [SHELL-06] WHEN a user activates Categories, THE Frontend SHALL open the Categories screen of the Board that is open, as before.
7. [SHELL-07] WHEN a user activates Inbox, or the bell in the App_Header, THE Frontend SHALL toggle the Side_Panel on the Inbox view through the same handler (Requirement 9 criterion 6, with "Top_Nav or Icon_Rail" for "Sidebar"). THE bell SHALL show the unread dot of V12 while the unread count is above zero.
8. [SHELL-08] WHEN a user activates Activity or Team, THE Frontend SHALL open the Side_Panel on that view; the Side_Panel shows one view at a time, a second activation of the open view closes it, and Escape closes it and returns focus to the item that opened it (Requirement 8 criterion 6, with "Top_Nav or Icon_Rail" for "Sidebar").

**Header and layout**

9. [SHELL-09] THE Frontend SHALL render the App_Header on every Protected_Route page (HDR-01), inside the app frame, as its first row. WHILE the viewport is 960 pixels or wider, THE App_Header SHALL hold, left to right, the `BrandLogo` link to `/boards`, the Top_Nav, a flexible gap, the bell (a 40 pixel icon button with the teal unread dot), and the User_Menu trigger (a 32 pixel avatar and a chevron); it SHALL NOT hold the page context, a search button, or a global search field (exclusions 1). WHILE the viewport is below 960 pixels, THE App_Header SHALL hold the menu button, the `BrandLogo` link, the bell, and the User_Menu trigger, and on a Board SHALL show the Board name in place of the wordmark as V12 does.
10. [SHELL-10] THE Frontend SHALL show `BrandLogo` with the variant `full` in the App_Header while the viewport is 960 pixels or wider, standing where the prototype draws its logo tile and wordmark, and with the variant `mark` below 960 pixels (Requirement 21, BRAND-07 and BRAND-10 as V15 rewords them); THE Icon_Rail SHALL NOT show the logo. THE Frontend SHALL NOT draw the prototype's lightning tile or the word RevoTask.
11. [SHELL-11] THE Frontend SHALL lay a page out as the prototype does: a `--surface-100` canvas with a `--page-x` margin on every side, the Icon_Rail at the left, and one app frame (white, radius `frame`, `--shadow-card`) holding the App_Header on top and the page below it. On a Board the page itself does not scroll (Requirement 14 criterion 18); the Columns scroll inside the frame; on every other page the frame's content scrolls and keeps `--page-x` padding. WHILE the viewport is below 960 pixels, THE frame SHALL fill the screen with no canvas margin and no radius, and there is no Icon_Rail.
12. [SHELL-12] WHEN a Board page is open, THE Frontend SHALL show in the Board_Toolbar, as its first element, the eyebrow "ACTIVE BOARD" (`text-2xs`, upper case, `--ink-muted`, with an `--accent` dot), and under it the Board_Switcher trigger: the Board name in `text-xl` with a chevron, on the Board_Switcher trigger recipe, with the accessible name "Switch board: <name>", opening a `BasePopover` panel on the popover recipe (reference `12-board-switcher-desktop.png`). Under the trigger a line `text-sm` `--ink-muted` reads "<n> Columns · <m> Total Tasks · <description>", from the Board payload, with the description omitted when empty.

**Board_Switcher**

13. [SHELL-13] THE Board_Switcher panel SHALL list every Board the viewer may see (Requirement 22 criterion 4) and, for each: the name, a role tag (`BaseBadge`) reading "Owner" when the viewer is the Board_Owner and otherwise the viewer's role, the card count ("<n> cards"), and the percentage done (`done_count` over `card_count`, rounded, shown as "<p>%", and "0%" when there are no Cards), from the `GET /api/boards` payload; THE panel SHALL mark the open Board with `aria-current="true"`, show a search field when the list has six or more Boards (Requirement 14 criterion 23), show no visibility icon (ACC-14) and no Email, and navigate to `/boards/{id}` on selection, closing the panel and returning focus to the trigger.
14. [SHELL-14] THE Frontend SHALL wrap a long Board name onto further lines in the Board_Switcher panel and the Board_Toolbar instead of cutting it off.
15. [SHELL-15] WHERE the viewer may create a Board (Requirement 3), THE Board_Switcher panel SHALL end with a "Create new board" button that opens the existing New Board modal; WHEN a Board is created, THE Frontend SHALL navigate to it as it does today.

**Board_Toolbar**

16. [SHELL-16] THE Board_Toolbar SHALL show, inside the filter bar at its right, the progress pill "<done> of <total> done (<p>%)" for the open Board while it has at least one Card: a white pill with a trend icon, the text with the percentage in `--accent`, and a 100 pixel meter (Requirement 38 criterion 18, Progress meter) with `role="progressbar"`, `aria-valuenow` the percentage, and an accessible name; THE figures come from the Board payload's `done_count` and `card_count` and are never computed from another source.
17. [SHELL-17] THE Board_Toolbar SHALL hold, at the right of the Board_Switcher row, the "Public link" and "Invite" quiet pill buttons (`--size-pill`, with an icon; "Invite" shows the pending join-request count as a teal count badge when the viewer decides on requests) with the same visibility rules and behaviour as before (Requirements 23, 24, and 31), icon-only below 960 pixels, and SHALL hold no other action: there is no board-level "Add Task" button (exclusions 17).
18. [SHELL-18] WHILE the viewport is between 360 and 414 pixels wide, THE Board_Toolbar SHALL wrap onto a second row so that nothing is cut off or overlaps, and the page SHALL NOT scroll sideways.

**Team view**

19. [SHELL-19] THE Side_Panel Team_View SHALL list the members of the open Board, the Board_Owner first with the "Owner" tag, then the others in the order of `GET /api/boards/{id}/members`; each row SHALL show the avatar through `BaseAvatar`, the full name, the Username muted, and the role tag of Requirement 30. The row SHALL show no Email except where Requirement 3 criterion 31 allows it.
20. [SHELL-20] WHERE the viewer is the Board_Owner, a Manager, or an Admin, THE Team_View SHALL show the member search field that adds a member and the remove action on each member, with the rules and confirmations of Requirements 3 and 22; a Member and a Supervisor who is not the owner see the list only.
21. [SHELL-21] WHEN an Admin activates Team, THE Frontend SHALL navigate to `/admin/users` instead of opening the Team_View, because an Admin has no Board access by default (Decision B, Open Question 40).

**Removal and boundaries**

22. [SHELL-22] THE Frontend SHALL remove the Sidebar, its collapse toggle, the stored collapse state, and its Boards list and Team members section; THE Frontend SHALL NOT keep a second navigation component beside the Icon_Rail, the Top_Nav, and the Nav_Drawer.
23. [SHELL-23] THE Frontend SHALL show no Log out item and no Profile entry in the Icon_Rail, the Top_Nav, or the Nav_Drawer; both stay in the User_Menu (HDR-02 to HDR-05).
24. [SHELL-24] THE Frontend SHALL show no Icon_Rail, no Top_Nav, no Nav_Drawer, and no Side_Panel on the sign-in pages, the invite pages' signed-out states, the session-expired route, and the Shared_View (SHARE-21 extended); the Shared_View keeps its own light header with `BrandLogo` and "Sign in".
25. [SHELL-25] THE Frontend SHALL make the first focusable element of every Protected_Route page a "Skip to content" link that moves focus to the main region, and SHALL order focus: skip link, App_Header, Icon_Rail, main region.
26. [SHELL-26] THE Side_Panel SHALL keep its behaviour (right side, one shared panel, closes on Escape) and SHALL be drawn as the prototype does (references `11-inbox-desktop.png` and `13-activity-desktop.png`): a floating white panel `--size-panel` wide, inset `--page-x` from the top, right, and bottom of the viewport, radius `frame`, `--shadow-panel`, over a blurred `--scrim` that also covers the Icon_Rail and the App_Header; below 960 pixels it fills the screen width. Its header is a 36 pixel icon tile (`--accent-tint`, radius `card`), the title ("Inbox", "Activity", or "Team") in `text-title`, an optional teal count badge, and a close button.
