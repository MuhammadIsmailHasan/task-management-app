# Verification, V16 (draft)

### Requirement 44: V16 Verification

**User Story:** As the product owner, I want automated checks that the design system, the shell, the landing page, the host split, and the throughput numbers stay correct, so that a later change cannot quietly break the look or the data.

This requirement extends Requirement 15. The checks below are written with the implementation tasks of group 38 and run in CI with the existing ones (Requirement 34).

#### Acceptance Criteria

**Style checks (Requirement 38)**

1. [V16-T01] THE Frontend SHALL keep every check of `frontend/tests/styling.test.ts` (no hex, no arbitrary value, no numeric `z-`, `duration-`, `opacity-`, `ring-`, `outline-`, or `border-` width outside the allowed set, no raw `<button>` outside `components/ui` and `components/shared`, no `max-*` variant, shadcn/ui primitives imported only from `components/shared` and `components/ui`) and SHALL add checks that: the landing files (`app/site/` and `components/landing/`) are scanned like every other file; no source uses a `rounded-*` utility outside `sm`, `control`, `card`, `surface`, and `full`; no source uses `font-*` family utilities other than the two token families; no source or stylesheet names Material Symbols, `fonts.googleapis.com`, or `fonts.gstatic.com`; and `tokens.css` no longer defines `--text-heading`, `--text-lg`, `--radius-md`, or `--radius-lg`.
2. [V16-T02] THE Frontend SHALL include a contrast test (Property 67) that parses `tokens.css`, computes the ratio of every pair listed in Requirement 38 criterion 7, and fails below 4.5 to 1 for text and 3 to 1 for the boundary and focus pairs.
3. [V16-T03] THE Frontend SHALL include a test that every row of the component recipe table (Requirement 38 criterion 18) is met by its `Base*` component: control heights equal `--size-control` and `--size-control-sm`, the radius role matches, and the type utility matches (Property 63).

**Shell (Requirement 39)**

4. [V16-T04] THE Frontend SHALL include component tests that: the Icon_Rail and the Top_Nav show the items of SHELL-05 for each of the roles Member, Supervisor, Manager, and Admin, both with and without an open Board; a rail item has a tooltip on hover and focus and none after a click; the Inbox item shows "9+" for ten or more unread; the Nav_Drawer opens from the menu button, traps focus, and closes on Escape, on the scrim, and on a route change; the Board_Switcher shows the search field only for six or more Boards, shows "Owner" or the viewer's role, the percentage done, and the card count, and navigates on selection; the Board_Toolbar progress meter shows the Board payload's figures and is absent for a Board with no Card; the Team_View lists the owner first with the role tags and shows the add and remove controls only to the Board_Owner, a Manager, and an Admin; an Admin's Team item goes to `/admin/users`; no Log out or Profile entry exists in the rail, the Top_Nav, or the drawer; and the "Skip to content" link is first in focus order.
5. [V16-T05] THE Frontend SHALL update every existing Playwright and `vitest` test that queries the Sidebar (for example the sign-out checks AUTH-E2E-SO and the Team members tests of V12) so that it queries the Icon_Rail, the Top_Nav, the Board_Switcher, or the Team_View and asserts the same behaviour; no test SHALL be deleted to make the suite pass.

**Screens (Requirement 40)**

6. [V16-T06] THE Frontend SHALL include tests that the register form has separate Username and Email fields with the availability check and has no Terms checkbox, no entropy meter, and no combined identifier field; that a board Card shows no identifier and no estimate; that the priority comment label reads "Priority"; that the Inbox shows no Mentions or Assignments tab; that the Activity view shows no filter, live indicator, or export; and that the Categories, Invite, Public link, Admin users, New Board, and Shared view screens show none of the excluded items of `specs/v16/00-OVERVIEW.md` that apply to them.
7. [V16-T07] THE Frontend SHALL include a Playwright check at 360, 414, 960, and 1280 pixels wide that, for the login page, the register page, Home, a Board, the Card modal, the Side_Panel, the Categories screen, the Dashboard, and the Landing_Page, the document has no horizontal scroll (`scrollWidth` not above `clientWidth`) and every icon-only control is at least 44 pixels in both directions below 960 pixels.

**Landing page and hosts (Requirements 41 and 42)**

8. [V16-T08] THE Frontend SHALL include tests that the Landing_Page renders the parts of LAND-02 in order with their `id` values; that its two call-to-action links use `APP_URL` when set and the relative paths otherwise; that its source imports no `fetch`, `cookies`, `headers`, or `localStorage` use; that it contains exactly one `h1`; that its text contains none of the words of exclusions 15 ("Pricing", "Enterprise", "PRO", "Viewer", "testimonial") and no "RevoTask"; and that the product preview is `inert`, and every number it shows equals the value the app's own functions compute from the sample data (Property 68).
9. [V16-T09] THE Frontend SHALL include the host routing property test of HOST-14 (Property 64) and unit tests for the cases: `SITE_URL` unset; a host with a port; an upper-case host; a host with a trailing dot; a spoofed `x-forwarded-host` that is malformed; and a request that carries a Host equal to the Site_Host with the path `/boards/abc?x=1`, which must redirect to `{APP_URL}/boards/abc?x=1`.
10. [V16-T10] THE smoke test SHALL carry the checks of HOST-13, and the CI workflow SHALL run it against the throwaway stack with `SITE_URL` and `APP_URL` set to test names.

**Throughput and Dashboard (Requirement 43)**

11. [V16-T11] THE Backend SHALL include tests, on PostgreSQL where the existing migration tests use it, that: the migration adds `completed_at`, backfills it from `updated_at` for Cards in `Done` only, leaves other Cards null, removes nothing, and downgrades cleanly; creating a Card in `Done`, moving a Card into, out of, and within `Done` sets, clears, and keeps `completed_at` (Property 66); soft-deleting a Card removes it from every count; the throughput lists have exactly `weeks` entries, oldest first, with zero weeks present, with Monday boundaries in `APP_TIMEZONE` including a Card completed at 23:59 Sunday and at 00:00 Monday; `total` equals the sum (Property 65); `weeks` of 0, 27, and "abc" return 400 `VALIDATION_ERROR` with `details.weeks`; roles return 200, 403, 403, 401, and 404 as THR-09 says; the query count is the same for 1 and for 30 Boards; and `done_delta` follows THR-04.
12. [V16-T12] THE Backend SHALL include a property test (Property 65) that, for any set of Cards with arbitrary `completed_at` values and any `weeks` from 1 to 26, each non-deleted Card with `completed_at` inside the window is counted in exactly one week, and the weekly values sum to the number of such Cards.
13. [V16-T13] THE Frontend SHALL include tests that the Dashboard redirects a Member and an Admin to `/boards`; selects the first Board on load; sums the cross-board figures for "All boards"; draws one bar per week with the hidden table; shows the Boards table to a Manager only; shows "Recent activity" for one Board and not for "All boards"; and shows the loading, error with Retry, and empty states.

**Regression and review**

14. [V16-T14] THE System SHALL pass `pytest`, `tsc --noEmit`, `vitest run`, and `next build` unchanged in command, with `backend/openapi.json` current (`test_committed_openapi_json_is_current`), before group 38 is ticked.
15. [V16-T15] THE implementer SHALL put each of the 13 prototype pages (the images of `design/reference/v2/`) beside the built screen for the owner at 1440 and 390 pixels and record the owner's decision on every difference in `specs/CHANGELOG.md` before group 38 is ticked; a difference the owner accepts becomes a V16 note, and one the owner rejects becomes a task.

16. [V16-T16] THE Frontend SHALL include a Playwright test of the recipe table (Requirement 38, DS-27) that renders each shared component once in a test page at 1440 pixels wide and asserts its computed height, border radius, padding, font size, font weight, and background colour against the token values of `tokens.css` for every row of criterion 18 that has them (for example: a primary button is 36 pixels high, fully rounded, `--accent` on `--on-accent`; a Board Card has radius `--radius-card`, padding 16 pixels, and `--surface-200`; a Column has radius `--radius-frame` and `--surface-100`; the Icon_Rail is 64 pixels wide and fully rounded; the app frame has radius `--radius-frame`). It SHALL also assert the hit area of Requirement 38 criterion 17: below 960 pixels every icon-only control and every pill shorter than 44 pixels has an interactive area of at least 44 by 44 pixels.
17. [V16-T17] THE Frontend SHALL include a Playwright run that opens the Board page, the Card modal, the New Board modal, the Board_Switcher, the Inbox, the Activity view, the auth page, and the Landing_Page at 1440 by 900 pixels, saves a screenshot of each to a git-ignored folder, and fails when a screenshot is blank or when the page shows an unstyled element (a computed font family other than the two token families, or a computed background and radius equal to the browser default on a recipe component). The owner compares these screenshots with `design/reference/v2/` in V16-T15; the test does not compare pixels, because the sample content differs.
