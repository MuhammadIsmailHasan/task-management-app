# Landing Page, V16 (draft)

### Requirement 41: Landing Page

**User Story:** As a visitor who has not heard of RevoBoard, I want one clear page that says what it is, what it does, and how to start, so that I can decide to create an account.

The page follows prototype page 2 ("Web Landing Page") in structure, tone, and look; the references are `02-landing-desktop-1.png` to `02-landing-desktop-3.png` and `02-landing-mobile.png`. It is built from the same Design_System and the same `Base*` components as the app (Decision AQ), so it has no styles of its own. Where the prototype describes something the product does not do, the page says only what is true (exclusions 15). This requirement resolves Open Question 36 and withdraws its proposal that a signed-in visitor is sent to `/boards`: the Site_Host cannot read the session of the App_Host (Requirement 42), so the page looks the same for everyone.

#### Acceptance Criteria

**Scope and delivery**

1. [LAND-01] THE Frontend SHALL serve the Landing_Page at `/` on the Site_Host as a Server Component that makes no call to the Backend, reads no cookie, and uses no `localStorage`, and whose only client-side script is the menu drawer of criterion 3 (Requirement 14 criterion 11).
2. [LAND-02] THE Landing_Page SHALL hold these parts in this order: header, hero with product preview, Features, Workflow, Teams, Roles, FAQ, closing call to action, footer. Each part from Features to FAQ SHALL have an `id` (`features`, `workflow`, `teams`, `roles`, `faq`) so the header can link to it.

**Header and calls to action**

3. [LAND-03] THE header SHALL be a full-width white bar of `--size-header` height at the top (reference `02-landing-desktop-1.png`) holding, left to right, `BrandLogo` `full` linking to `/`, the anchors Features, Workflow, Teams, and FAQ as plain `text-sm` medium links in `--ink-muted` (the active anchor in `--ink`), a flexible gap, a "Sign in" text link, and a "Get started" dark pill button (`--brand`, `--size-button`, `--shadow-card`); below 960 pixels it holds a menu button that opens a `BaseDrawer` with the same anchors and the same two buttons. The header is sticky.
4. [LAND-04] THE "Sign in" button SHALL link to `{APP_URL}/login` and THE "Get started" button SHALL link to `{APP_URL}/register`, where `APP_URL` is the App_Host origin (Requirement 42 criterion 6). No other link on the page SHALL leave the page except these two and the footer links of criterion 12.

**Hero**

5. [LAND-05] UNDER the header SHALL sit an announcement bar (`--brand` fill, 40 pixels high, centred `text-sm` text in `--on-brand`) holding a teal count-style pill reading "RevoBoard", one line of true copy, "Drag-and-drop boards with roles, share links, and join requests.", and an arrow link "Get started" in `--accent-tint`. THE hero under it SHALL be centred: one `h1` in `text-display` reading "Your workflow, your way." with "Master tasks effortlessly." in `--accent` on the same line from 960 pixels and on the next line below, one paragraph of `text-base` `--ink-muted` copy of at most two sentences (the prototype has none; the page may omit it), the "Get started" dark pill enlarged to 44 pixels high with an arrow icon, and the product preview of criterion 6. It SHALL show no "NEW" chip, no rating, and no customer logos.
6. [LAND-06] THE product preview SHALL render a sample Board inside a window frame (radius `frame`, `--surface-200`, `--border`, `--shadow-float`, padding 24) with the app's own Column and Card components, from fixed sample data held in one typed file under `frontend/lib/` that uses the Backend's Card and Board types. The frame has a top row with three window dots (`--tone-red`, `--tone-orange`, `--tone-green`), the Board name, and at the right the pills "Private board" and "Public link on". Under it a grid of four columns from 960 pixels: a brand card (`--brand` with `--on-brand`, radius `card`) showing the derived board progress and the subtask count with a meter, and a quiet card for the pending join request with Approve and Reject icon buttons; two Columns (In Progress and Review) with their Cards, one Card with subtasks and its derived progress and one overdue Card; and a "Workflow columns" card listing the five Columns with the Card count of each. Every number shown (counts, subtask ratios, percentages) SHALL be computed from the sample data by the same functions the app uses (Requirement 27), so the preview cannot disagree with itself. The preview SHALL be inert (`inert` and `aria-hidden`), have a visible text alternative "Illustration of a RevoBoard board" for assistive technology, and below 960 pixels show the brand card and the first Column only.

**Content**

7. [LAND-07] THE Features part SHALL carry the eyebrow "WHAT YOU GET" (`text-2xs`, upper case, `--accent`), a centred `text-xl` title, one centred `text-base` line, and four feature cards in a row from 1280 pixels (two columns from 960, one below) on the Landing feature card recipe, each with a 48 pixel icon tile (radius `card`; `--accent-tint`, `--brand`, `--surface-sunken`, and `--accent-tint` in turn), a `text-page-title` heading, up to three lines of `text-sm` copy, and a footer line with a one pixel `--border` above it and an arrow link text in `--accent` or `--brand`: (a) drag and drop across five Columns, with the position kept after a refresh; (b) private Boards with a read-only public link and an invite link with join requests; (c) subtasks whose checklist sets the Card's progress; (d) roles, threaded comments, and an activity log. It MAY add a fifth and sixth card for categories with filters and for assignment and deadline notifications, because both exist.
8. [LAND-08] THE Workflow part SHALL show the five default Columns as a numbered row with one short line each: Todo (planned), In Progress (being worked on), Review (waiting for a check), Revision (changes requested), Done (finished). It SHALL NOT claim that a Card moves by itself.
9. [LAND-09] THE Teams part SHALL carry the heading "Private by default. Share when you need to." and these points, and no others: every Board is private to its owner and members; a public link shows the Board read-only, without comments and activity; an invite link sends a join request that the Board's owner, a Manager, or an Admin approves or rejects; every Card move, edit, and comment is recorded in an activity log. From 960 pixels the part is two columns as the prototype draws it (`02-landing-desktop-2.png`): at the left an inert sample card (`--surface-200`, radius `frame`, `--shadow-card`) showing two sample join requests with their Approve and Reject buttons, built from the app's own components and sample data; at the right the eyebrow "SECURE TEAM WORKFLOWS", the heading in `text-xl`, one `text-base` paragraph, and two small cards ("Join request queue" and "Activity history", radius `card`) with an icon, a `text-sm` semibold title, and one line. The prototype's sample card claims features the product does not have (a role-permission checklist and "Revision triggers") and SHALL NOT be copied.
10. [LAND-10] THE Roles part SHALL show one card per role with a line that matches Requirement 2: Member (move and edit Cards, comment), Supervisor (adds create, assign, archive, and delete Cards, Categories, and analytics for their Board), Manager (adds analytics across all Boards, creating Boards, and flagging priority comments), Admin (manages user accounts), and a fifth card "Guest" (reads a Board through a public link). It SHALL NOT name roles the product does not have (for example Owner, Viewer, or Enterprise).
11. [LAND-11] THE FAQ part SHALL show these four questions as native `<details>` elements, in this order: which columns a Board has; how private Boards and join requests work; how a Card's progress is calculated; which roles exist. Each answer SHALL agree with Requirements 2, 3, 24, and 27 and with criteria 8 to 10. The items sit in one centred column at most `--container-copy` wide on the FAQ item recipe, each with a chevron at the right that turns when it is open.
12. [LAND-12] THE closing call to action SHALL be a `--gradient-cta` band (the CTA band recipe) holding one `text-xl` line, "Ready to organize your team?", at the left and, at the right, a "Get started" pill in `--accent-tint` with `--on-accent-tint` text; a second centred call to action under the FAQ SHALL show a 64 pixel `--accent-tint` circle with a rocket icon, the line "Ready to master your workflow?", one `text-base` line, and the two buttons ("Get started" dark, "Sign in" quiet). THE footer SHALL be a `--surface-100` band holding `BrandLogo` `full` with one `text-sm` line, three link columns (Product: Features, Workflow, Teams; Roles: the four roles; Account: Sign in, Create account) that link to anchors and to the App_Host only, and a bottom row "© <current year> RevoBoard". The page SHALL NOT link to pricing, terms, privacy, blog, or contact pages, because none exists, and SHALL NOT show a "Web App Operational" status.

**Truth, search, and quality**

13. [LAND-13] EVERY claim on the Landing_Page SHALL be true of the product as specified in Requirements 1 to 43; a claim that names a feature listed in the exclusions SHALL NOT appear; and the page SHALL show no pricing, tier name, testimonial, usage statistic, or security certification.
14. [LAND-14] THE Landing_Page SHALL set the title `RevoBoard`, a meta description of at most 160 characters, `lang="en"`, a canonical link to `{SITE_URL}/`, and Open Graph tags (`og:title`, `og:description`, `og:type` `website`, `og:url`, `og:site_name`); and THE Site_Host SHALL serve `/robots.txt` allowing all and naming `{SITE_URL}/sitemap.xml`, and a `/sitemap.xml` listing `/` only. THE App_Host SHALL serve a `/robots.txt` that disallows all and every App_Host page SHALL send `X-Robots-Tag: noindex`.
15. [LAND-15] THE Landing_Page SHALL have one `h1`, headings in order without skipped levels, the landmarks `header`, `main`, and `footer`, a visible focus ring on every link and button, a "Skip to content" link as its first focusable element, and the contrast of Requirement 38 criterion 7; anchor jumps SHALL stop below the sticky header through a scroll margin equal to `--size-header`.
16. [LAND-16] WHILE the viewport is between 360 and 414 pixels wide, THE Landing_Page SHALL show every part stacked in one column without horizontal scroll or cut-off content, with touch targets of at least 44 pixels.
17. [LAND-17] THE Landing_Page SHALL load only the two self-hosted font families, the `BrandLogo` files, and the page's own script and style, SHALL contain no image other than the `BrandLogo` files, and SHALL make no third-party request.
18. [LAND-18] THE Landing_Page SHALL use only Design_System tokens and the `Base*` components, and SHALL pass the style checks of Requirement 44 (DS-24).
