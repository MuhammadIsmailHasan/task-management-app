# Screens, V16 (draft)

### Requirement 40: Screen Restyle

**User Story:** As a user, I want every existing screen to look like the prototype, so that the product feels like one design while it keeps every feature it has today.

This requirement changes how existing screens look and where their controls sit. It adds no feature except where another V16 requirement says so. Every behaviour, permission, error message, and API call of Requirements 1 to 37 stands unless a criterion below says otherwise. Each screen is specified against its reference render in `design/reference/v2/` (Requirement 38 criterion 25), and all sizes, radii, fills, and type come from the recipe table of Requirement 38 criterion 18; this requirement fixes what goes where.

#### Acceptance Criteria

**All screens**

1. [SCR-01] THE Frontend SHALL restyle each screen below only through the Design_System (Requirement 38): the component recipes, the colour, type, radius, and shadow tokens, and `lucide-react` icons. A screen SHALL NOT keep a V15 or earlier value that Requirement 38 replaced (the 6 pixel card radius, the `#13226b` brand, the `#f3f4fa` page colour, the 16 and 17 pixel text sizes, the 40 pixel button height).
2. [SCR-02] THE Frontend SHALL keep each screen's content, order, empty state, loading state, error state, and Retry control, and SHALL restyle them with the same recipes; a state the prototype does not draw (empty, loading, error, confirm) is drawn from the same recipes and not left in its old style.
3. [SCR-03] THE Frontend SHALL keep every screen free of horizontal scroll and cut-off content between 360 and 414 pixels wide, with the 44 pixel hit area of Requirement 38 criterion 17 on every small control. The prototype's mobile renders (`*-mobile.png`) keep the 64 pixel rail and scroll sideways; V16 does not copy that: below 960 pixels the rail becomes the Nav_Drawer (SHELL-04) and the app frame fills the screen (SHELL-11).

**Sign-in and register [page 5, `05-auth`]**

4. [SCR-04] THE Frontend SHALL draw `/login` and `/register` as one auth card centred on the canvas, at most 1152 pixels wide (`--container-site` clamps it), radius `frame`, `--shadow-panel`, in two halves from 960 pixels. The left half is the auth brand panel (`--gradient-brand`, two soft `--accent-tint` glow circles behind the text): `BrandLogo` `full` on a translucent white chip at the top left, the `text-display` line "Work in flow, scale with clarity." under it, and at the bottom a thin divider and one `text-sm` line in `--on-brand-muted`. The right half holds, from the top: the segmented switch ("Sign in" and "Create account", each a link to its own route, Requirement 1), the form title in `text-xl`, one `text-sm` `--ink-muted` line, the fields, the submit button, and the security note row. Below 960 pixels the brand panel is a compact band above the form inside the same card.
5. [SCR-05] THE register form SHALL keep the separate full name, Username, Email, and password fields with the blur availability check and the 8 character rule (Requirement 1, USR-07 and USR-08). Each field is a labelled input (label `text-meta` semibold above, a leading `lucide-react` icon inside at the left) on the Input recipe; "Available" or the taken message shows as a status pill beside the Username and Email labels; the password field has a show or hide eye button inside at the right. THE form SHALL NOT show a Terms checkbox, an "Entropy score", a "Workspace Ready" chip, or a single "Username or work email" field (exclusions 7).
6. [SCR-06] THE sign-in form SHALL keep the Username-or-email field and the password field with the show or hide toggle, the 429 countdown, and the Safe_Return_Path, on the same recipes as criterion 5.

**Home and board [pages 1, 10, 12, `01-board`, `11-create-board`, `13-board-switcher`]**

7. [SCR-07] THE Home page (`/boards`) SHALL sit in the app frame under the App_Header with a `text-title` title "Boards", the analytics strip of Requirement 10 criterion 8 as KPI tiles in a row above (Supervisor and Manager only), then the Boards as Home tiles in a grid of one column below 960 pixels, two from 960, and three from 1280; each tile shows the name in `text-page-title`, up to two lines of description, the progress meter with the percentage, the card count, and the member avatars (up to four, then a "+N" count badge). It SHALL show no "Welcome Back" hero.
8. [SCR-08] THE Board page SHALL show, inside the app frame under the App_Header and in this order: the Board_Toolbar (SHELL-12, SHELL-16, SHELL-17) with the Board_Switcher row at the left and the "Public link" and "Invite" pills at the right; the filter bar (radius `frame`, `--surface-100`, padding 12) holding at its left the filter chips ("All Tasks" first, active chip filled `--brand`, then one chip per category) and at its right the progress pill and the search field (a white 40 pixel field with a leading search icon, "Search tasks..."), wrapping below 960 pixels; then the Columns in a row that scrolls sideways inside the frame. Each Column is a Column recipe: a header with the tone dot (`--size-dot-md`), the name in `text-page-title`, the card count badge, and the Column menu button at the right; the Cards stacked with `--gutter` between them; and the "Add Card" button at the foot. There is no "Add Task" button and no "Welcome Back" hero (exclusions 2 and 17).
9. [SCR-09] A board Card SHALL be laid out as the prototype draws it: a first row with the category tag at the left and the priority pill at the right; the title (`text-card-title`, up to three lines); up to two lines of description (`text-sm` `--ink-muted`); the progress bar and the subtask count only when the Card has subtasks (Requirement 27); and a footer row of `text-meta` `--ink-muted` items with a 14 pixel icon each (subtasks as done over total, the end date, and the comment and attachment counts when above zero) and the single assignee avatar (28 pixels) at the right. An overdue Card shows its end date in `--danger` with an alert icon and a `--danger` progress bar. A Card in the `Done` Column shows its title with a line through it in `--ink-muted`, a "Completed" pill (`--accent-tint`) in place of the priority pill, and its subtask count in `--accent`. It SHALL NOT show a Card identifier, an estimate, a second assignee, or text the Card does not store ("Re-opened", "Needs QA redo", "PR #842"; exclusions 4 to 6).
10. [SCR-10] THE Frontend SHALL keep drag and drop with its placeholder and the Column auto-scroll (Requirement 5) and SHALL draw a dragged Card with `--shadow-dragging` and the same radius as at rest; the drop placeholder is a `--surface-sunken` block of the Card's height with the Card radius.
11. [SCR-11] THE Card modal SHALL be laid out as the prototype draws it (`04-card-modal-desktop.png`): a `BaseModal` about 900 pixels wide with two panes divided by a one pixel `--border`. Its header holds a breadcrumb (the Board name, a slash, the Column name as a teal pill), an auto-save status pill ("Auto-saved just now" with a cloud icon), and the close button. The left pane holds the eyebrow "CARD TITLE" (`text-2xs`), the title field (`text-xl`), a row of attribute chips (category, priority, dates, assignee; each a `--surface-100` pill of `--size-control-sm` height with a label and a value, opening its popover), the Description section with its helper line, the Subtasks section (a title, the "<n> of <m> done (<p>%)" pill in `--accent-tint`, the progress bar, one `--surface-100` row of radius `field` per subtask with a checkbox and a drag handle, and "Add subtask"), and the Attachments section (file cards of radius `card` with a type tile, the name, the size and age, and a delete button; "Upload file" and "Add link" at the right of the section title). The right pane holds the Discussion title with the comment count, the thread, and the composer pinned at the foot of the pane; the Card's activity list sits under the thread under the eyebrow "ACTIVITY LOG". The footer holds "Created <date> by <name>" at the left and the Archive (quiet) and Delete (danger) buttons at the right. THE Card modal SHALL keep every behaviour of Requirements 4, 7, 25, 26, and 27. The comment marked as priority SHALL be labelled "Priority", not "Pinned"; the composer has no mention, emoji, or attachment control; the modal has no Save, Done, bookmark, or share button.
12. [SCR-12] THE Card modal SHALL show the assignee chip with one avatar and the full name, and the role tag beside each comment author (Requirement 30), and SHALL show a `--accent` check icon on a completed subtask row with the text in `--ink-muted` and struck through, as the prototype does.

**Side panel [pages 11 and 13, `12-inbox`, `13-activity`]**

13. [SCR-13] THE Inbox view SHALL keep the list of Notifications grouped by day, each row a link (Requirement 28), "Mark all as read", and "Load more", drawn as the prototype does: under the header a segmented switch (only "All" and "Unread", each with its count; the Mentions and Assignments tabs are excluded), then a day label (an `--accent` dot, the day in `text-2xs`, and the update count at the right), then the Notification rows on the Panel row recipe: a 36 pixel avatar with a small type badge at its corner, the actor name in `text-sm` semibold, the time at the right, the action line with the Card or Board name in semibold, an `--accent` unread dot, and, on a `board_join_requested` row where Requirement 24 allows it, the Approve (primary pill) and Decline (quiet pill) buttons. It SHALL NOT show quoted comment text, "Reply", "View Task", "Clear Read", or "Notification Settings" (exclusions 9); the whole row is the link.
14. [SCR-14] THE Activity view SHALL keep the Board activity feed with "Load more" and the existing wording of each action (Requirement 8), drawn as the prototype does: a day label ("TODAY", "EARLIER"), then one row per entry on the Panel row recipe with a 28 pixel actor avatar, the actor name semibold, the time at the right, the action line, and, for a move, the two Column names as pills with an arrow between them. It SHALL NOT show the "Live Stream" chip, the events, active-users, and system-state tiles, the filter field and filter chips, an export button, or a retention line (exclusions 10).

**Management screens [pages 6, 8, 9, `06-invite-public-link`, `08-user-management`, `09-categories`]**

15. [SCR-15] THE Categories screen (`/boards/{id}/categories`) SHALL stay a per-Board add, rename, recolour, and delete screen (Requirement 6), drawn in the prototype's layout: a title block (eyebrow, `text-xl` title "Categories", one line of `text-sm` copy) with the "New category" primary pill at its right, then one table card (radius `frame`, `--surface-200`) whose header row is `text-2xs` upper case labels and whose rows hold a colour swatch (the category's `--label-*` colour as a 16 pixel circle), the name in `text-sm` semibold, and Edit and Delete icon buttons at the right; the create and edit form is a `BaseModal` with a name field and a row of swatches. It SHALL NOT show KPI tiles, the quick-filter chips, "Associated boards", "Automated rules", "Import", an archived toggle, or a hex-code column (exclusions of V15).
16. [SCR-16] THE Invite modal and the Public link modal SHALL keep the controls and rules of Requirements 23 and 24, drawn in the prototype's layout (`06-invite-public-link-desktop.png`): a `BaseModal` with a 3 pixel `--accent` to `--brand` strip at its top edge, an icon tile and the title "Board access" with the Board name as a pill and one line of copy, a two-item segmented switch ("Invite link" with the pending count badge, and "Public link" with an `--accent` dot while it is on), then the active tab: the link card (`--surface-100`, radius `card`) with a status pill ("Active"), the read-only link field (a leading link icon and a monospace `text-sm` value) and a "Copy link" dark pill, then "Regenerate" (quiet) and "Disable" (danger) with the one-line warning; under it, for the Invite tab, the "Pending join requests" section with its count pill and one row per request (a 40 pixel avatar, the full name, the Username muted, the Email where Requirement 24 allows it, the request age, and the Approve and Reject pills). The footer holds a "Done" dark pill. It SHALL NOT show view counts, "CDN Cached", domain restrictions, a department tag, an online dot, a "Settings" link, an "Access logs audited" line, or an invite role picker (exclusions 12).
17. [SCR-17] THE Admin users screen (`/admin/users`) SHALL keep list, create, edit, and role change under the rules of Requirement 1 (criteria 7 and 8 and USR-12), drawn in the prototype's layout (`08-user-management-desktop.png`): a title block with the "Invite member" primary pill replaced by "Add user" (it opens the existing create form), a toolbar card (radius `card`, `--surface-100`) holding the search field and the role filter chips ("All", "Admins", "Members", and so on), then one table card whose header row is `text-2xs` upper case labels and whose rows hold the avatar, the full name and Email, the role as a role tag, and an Edit icon button; below 960 pixels the rows stack as cards. It SHALL NOT show the three summary tiles, seat quota, "Upgrade License", 2FA, Suspended and Invited states, "Last active", a "Board access" column, "Export Directory", or the multi-email invitation (exclusions 13).
18. [SCR-18] THE New Board modal SHALL be drawn as the prototype does (`11-create-board-desktop.png`): a `BaseModal` about 480 pixels wide with the title "Create new board" and one line of copy, a "Board name" field with a required mark, an optional "Description" textarea, a "Default workflow columns" row that shows the five Column names as white pills in a `--surface-100` bar (read-only), and a footer with the Cancel (quiet) and "Create board" (primary) pills. It SHALL NOT show an owner select (exclusions 14).

**Public and profile [page 7, `07-public-board`]**

19. [SCR-19] THE Shared_View (`/shared/{token}`) SHALL stay the read-only Board, drawn as the prototype does: inside the app frame, a banner row (`--accent-tint` fill, "Public read-only view" in `--on-accent-tint` and, at the right, the "Sign in to join" link when the viewer is signed out), a header with `BrandLogo` `full`, a "Public" pill, and a "Sign in" button; under it the Board title block (a lock tile, the Board name in `text-xl`, a "Public view" pill, the description), the filter chips and search field of Requirement 12 with the progress pill, and the Columns and Cards of criterion 8 and 9 without the assignee eye icon and with no drag handle. The Card modal opens read-only without comments and activity (Requirement 18). It SHALL NOT show the Icon_Rail, the Top_Nav, the Overview, Roadmap, and Team tabs, an export button, "Live synced", "Request Access", or card identifiers (exclusions 4 and 11).
20. [SCR-20] THE Profile page SHALL keep the two-column layout from 960 pixels (Requirement 29) inside the app frame, with each section a card of radius `frame` on `--surface-100`, the fields on the Input recipe, and the avatar section drawn with a 64 pixel avatar; the prototype draws no profile page.
21. [SCR-21] THE invite page (`/invite/{token}`) and the "link not valid", session-expired, and not-found pages SHALL be restyled as one centred card (radius `frame`, `--surface-200`, `--shadow-popover`, padding 32) on the `--surface-100` canvas with `BrandLogo` `full`, keeping their text and actions.

**Titles, brand, and states**

22. [SCR-22] THE Frontend SHALL keep every document title of Requirement 32 (`RevoBoard | <name>`) and SHALL name the product RevoBoard everywhere, never RevoTask.
23. [SCR-23] THE Frontend SHALL draw every empty, loading, and error state with the same recipes: an empty list as a centred icon tile (`--accent-tint`, radius `card`), a `text-page-title` line, and one `text-sm` `--ink-muted` line; a loading list as `--surface-strong` skeleton blocks with the radius of the thing they stand for; an error as the same block with the error text in `--danger` and a quiet "Retry" pill.
24. [SCR-24] THE Frontend SHALL draw every confirmation (`BaseConfirm`, Requirement 26) as a popover on the popover recipe with the question, a quiet "Cancel" pill, and a danger or primary confirm pill, and every toast and inline message as a `--surface-100` bar of radius `field` with an icon, with `--danger-soft` and `--accent-tint` as the error and success fills.
