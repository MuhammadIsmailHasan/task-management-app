# Paths and Navigation, V17 (draft)
### Requirement 46: Path Topology
**User Story:** As the product owner, I want the landing page and the app on one origin, with the app under `/app`, so that one domain, one certificate, and one deploy serve everything.
This requirement supersedes Requirement 42 (HOST-01 to HOST-15) and amends Requirement 33 (DEP-05 and DEP-13) and Requirement 34 (the smoke test). One Next.js process serves `/` and `/app/...`. The Backend, the database, and the Flask routes do not change; only nginx and the public URL prefix do (Decision AZ).
#### Acceptance Criteria
**Routes**
1. [PATH-01] THE Frontend SHALL serve the Landing_Page at `/` and every other page under `/app`: `/app/boards`, `/app/b/{slug}`, `/app/b/{slug}/list`, `/app/b/{slug}/calendar`, `/app/b/{slug}/archived`, `/app/dashboard`, `/app/my-tasks`, `/app/users`, `/app/profile`, `/app/login`, `/app/register`, `/app/verify`, `/app/invite/{token}`, `/app/shared/{token}`, `/app/session-expired`, and `/app/boards/{id}` (the redirect-only route of PATH-08). THE Frontend SHALL implement this with route folders (`app/app/...`), not with Next.js `basePath`, because the Landing_Page lives outside `/app`.
2. [PATH-02] THE production build SHALL set `NEXT_PUBLIC_API_URL` to `/app/api`. THE Frontend SHALL build every Backend call as `{NEXT_PUBLIC_API_URL}/...` (for example `/app/api/boards`). IN development, `next.config.ts` SHALL rewrite `/app/api/:path*` to the Backend's `/api/:path*`. Server-side calls keep using the internal Backend URL (Requirement 33).
3. [PATH-03] nginx SHALL proxy `/app/api/` to the Flask `/api/` routes with the prefix removed (`proxy_pass` with a trailing slash), SHALL send every other `/app` path and `/` to the Frontend, SHALL answer HTTP 404 for `/api/` on the public side so that the Backend is reachable at one path only, and SHALL keep the request body limit of Requirement 33. THE Flask routes SHALL NOT change.
4. [PATH-04] THE Backend SHALL build every absolute path it returns with the configured public prefix `API_PUBLIC_PREFIX` (default empty in development and testing, `/app/api` in production): the `file_url` and `avatar_url` of signed files (Requirement 19, the attachment rules of `backend.md`), and the `path` of the Invite_Link and the Public_Link, which become `/app/invite/{token}` and `/app/shared/{token}`. THE signature of a Signed_File_URL SHALL cover the file, the scope, and the expiry, and SHALL NOT cover the prefix, so a change of prefix invalidates no valid URL.
**Host routing removed**
5. [PATH-05] THE Frontend SHALL remove `lib/host.ts`, `lib/origins.ts`, the host branch of `proxy.ts`, the environment variables `SITE_URL` and `APP_URL`, and `app/site/`; THE Landing_Page SHALL move to `app/page.tsx`. THE `proxy.ts` matcher SHALL cover `/app/:path*`. THE guard SHALL protect every path under `/app` except `/app/login`, `/app/register`, `/app/verify`, `/app/session-expired`, the signed-out states of `/app/invite/{token}` (which show the sign-in prompt), and `/app/shared/{token}`. A request without a valid Session_Cookie to a guarded path SHALL redirect to `/app/login?next=<safe path>` and remove the cookie. THE `next` value SHALL be used only when it is a local path under `/app/b/`, `/app/boards`, `/app/dashboard`, `/app/my-tasks`, `/app/users`, `/app/profile`, or `/app/invite/`.
6. [PATH-06] THE Session_Cookie SHALL stay host-only (no `Domain`) and SHALL gain `Path=/app`, so the Landing_Page and every other path never receive it. WHEN TLS is enabled the cookie SHALL be `Secure`. The Landing_Page SHALL still read no cookie and make no Backend call (LAND-01).
**Search engines**
7. [PATH-07] THE `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` routes SHALL name `/` only; THE sitemap SHALL list no `/app` URL. EVERY response under `/app` SHALL carry `X-Robots-Tag: noindex`, set in `proxy.ts`. THE `robots.txt` SHALL NOT disallow `/app`, because a disallowed page is never fetched and its `noindex` header would never be read (Open Question 67). THE V12 headers `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer` and `X-Robots-Tag: noindex` of the Shared_View and the invite page stay.
**Old paths**
8. [PATH-08] THE Frontend SHALL answer these paths with HTTP 308 to the `/app` equivalent, keeping the query string: `/boards` to `/app/boards`, `/boards/{id}` to `/app/boards/{id}`, `/dashboard` to `/app/dashboard`, `/admin/users` to `/app/users`, `/profile` to `/app/profile`, `/login` to `/app/login`, `/register` to `/app/register`, `/invite/{token}` to `/app/invite/{token}`, `/shared/{token}` to `/app/shared/{token}`, and `/session-expired` to `/app/session-expired`. THE route `/app/boards/{id}` SHALL read the Board through `GET /api/boards/{id}` and redirect to `/app/b/{slug}` (with the same query), and SHALL show the Not_Found_Page when the Backend answers 404 and the Board_Unavailable page when it answers 403 (Open Question 65).
9. [PATH-09] THE Frontend SHALL NOT redirect an old Board slug (Requirement 48, OQ46).
**Internal links**
10. [PATH-10] THE Frontend SHALL build every internal link and every `router.push` from one module, `lib/routes.ts`, which exports `appPath(...)` helpers (`boardPath(slug)`, `boardViewPath(slug, view)`, `cardPath(slug, cardId)`, `sharedPath(token)`, `invitePath(token)`, and so on). No component SHALL write the string `/app/` or `/boards` by hand. A test SHALL scan `components/` and `app/` for hand-written route strings and fail on a match outside `lib/routes.ts`. THE Inbox `hrefFor` function SHALL use these helpers.
**Deployment**
11. [PATH-11] THE deployment files SHALL change as follows (tasks 39.9 and 39.48): `deploy/nginx/revoboard.conf` (one server block, `/app/api/` proxy, `/api/` 404, no host names beyond the optional `server_name`), `compose.yaml` and `deploy/bare/supervisor.conf` (remove `SITE_URL` and `APP_URL`; add `NEXT_PUBLIC_API_URL` at build time and `API_PUBLIC_PREFIX` for the Backend), `deploy/deploy.sh`, `deploy/seed.sh`, `deploy/setup-server.sh`, `deploy/bare/*.sh`, `deploy/smoke.sh` and `deploy/smoke.mjs` (check `/` returns the Landing_Page, `/app/login` returns 200, `/app/api/health` returns 200, `/api/health` returns 404, `/boards` answers 308 to `/app/boards`, and an unknown `/app/xyz` returns 404 with the Not_Found_Page), and `deploy/README.md` (the new topology, the 308 table, the cookie path). THE access by server IP and port 8082 SHALL keep working, because no host name is required (DEP-13).
12. [PATH-12] THE CORS allow-list (`ALLOWED_ORIGINS`) SHALL stay and list the one public origin; the browser makes only same-origin calls, so CORS applies to development only. THE Landing_Page SHALL keep the rules of Requirement 41 (static, no API call) with its two call-to-action links pointing to `/app/register` and `/app/login`.
### Requirement 47: Navigation
**User Story:** As a signed-in user, I want global places in a left sidebar and the places of the open Board in a menu at the top, so that I always know where I am and what belongs to the Board.
This requirement supersedes Requirement 39 for the navigation items (SHELL-01 to SHELL-08 and SHELL-17 to SHELL-21), keeps its App_Header (HDR rules), Board_Switcher (SHELL-12 to SHELL-15), progress pill (SHELL-16), Skip link (SHELL-25), and Side_Panel look (SHELL-26), and amends Requirements 8, 9, and 6 where they say where a view opens (Decision BA). The Sidebar here is new; it is not the V1 to V15 Sidebar. Reference: `design/reference/v2/01-board-desktop.png` for the frame, the Side_Panel references `11-inbox-desktop.png`, `13-activity-desktop.png`, and `09-categories-desktop.png` for the panel views.
#### Acceptance Criteria
**Sidebar (global)**
1. [NAV-01] ON every Protected_Route page, THE Frontend SHALL show a left Sidebar with these items, in this order, each only to the roles named: Dashboard (Supervisor and Manager, Requirement 10), My Tasks (Member, Supervisor, and Manager; not an Admin, who cannot be an assignee, TASK-04), Boards (every signed-in user), and Users (Admin only). An item that is not shown takes no space. THE items come from one list, `shell/navItems.ts` (NAV-05).
2. [NAV-02] THE Sidebar SHALL have a toggle with the accessible name "Collapse sidebar" or "Expand sidebar" and `aria-expanded`. EXPANDED, it shows each item's icon and label; COLLAPSED, it shows icons only, and each item has a `BaseTooltip` with its label that shows on hover and on keyboard focus only (never after a click). THE default is expanded (OQ48). THE state SHALL be stored per browser under one `localStorage` key and read safely: a missing key, an unknown value, a storage error, or a server render SHALL give expanded. A change of the state SHALL NOT move focus.
3. [NAV-03] WHILE the viewport is below 960 pixels, THE Frontend SHALL hide the Sidebar and show in the App_Header a menu button (44 pixels, accessible name "Open menu", `aria-expanded`) that opens the Nav_Drawer: an off-canvas panel from the left, built as `BaseDrawer`, behind a scrim, listing the Sidebar items as 44 pixel rows and, while a Board is open, a second group "This board" with the Top_Menu items (NAV-04). THE Nav_Drawer SHALL trap focus, close on Escape, on a scrim click, and on a route change, and return focus to the menu button.
**Top_Menu (board only)**
4. [NAV-04] WHILE a Board is open (`/app/b/{slug}` and its views), THE App_Header SHALL show the Top_Menu with these items in this order: Board, List, Calendar, Activity, Members, Categories, and Share. Board, List, and Calendar are routes (`/app/b/{slug}`, `/list`, `/calendar`), have `aria-current="page"` when active, and keep the search and filter query of the current view where it applies. Activity, Members, and Categories open the right Side_Panel on that view; a second activation of the open view closes it. Share opens the Share_Modal (Requirement 55). WHICH roles see which item: Board, List, Calendar, Activity, and Members, every user with Board_Access; Categories, a Supervisor, a Manager, and an Admin (Requirement 6); Share, the Board_Owner, a Manager, and an Admin. THE Top_Menu SHALL NOT appear on any page that is not a Board view, and the Archived page (`/app/b/{slug}/archived`) SHALL be reached from the Board toolbar's "Archived" link and show the Top_Menu with Board active.
5. [NAV-05] THE Top_Menu is the main menu of a Board; THE Sidebar SHALL NOT repeat a Top_Menu item. THE Frontend SHALL keep both lists in one module, `shell/navItems.ts`, exporting `sidebarItems` and `topMenuItems`, each with an `isVisible({ role, boardOpen, isOwner })` predicate and an action (a route or a Side_Panel view or the Share_Modal), so the Sidebar, the Top_Menu, and the Nav_Drawer never disagree. A test SHALL assert that no id appears in both lists.
**Bell**
6. [NAV-06] THE Frontend SHALL reach notifications only from the bell in the App_Header. THE bell opens the Side_Panel on the Inbox view, drawn as `11-inbox-desktop.png`, and shows the unread dot (V12) and the count badge capped at "9+" while the unread count is above zero. THERE SHALL be no Inbox item in the Sidebar, the Top_Menu, or the Nav_Drawer.
**Side_Panel views**
7. [NAV-07] THE Side_Panel SHALL show one view at a time from `inbox | activity | members | categories`. THE Categories view replaces the Categories screen (`/boards/{id}/categories`) and holds the same add, rename, recolor, and delete controls and the same role rules (Requirement 6), with creates and deletes through ConfirmDialog; THE Members view is the Team_View of V16 (SHELL-19 and SHELL-20) with the member search, remove action, role tags, and the "Owner" tag unchanged, and is shown to an Admin too (Open Question 61). THE panel keeps SHELL-26 (floating, scrim, Escape closes and returns focus to the opener). Opening a view SHALL set and Escape or close SHALL restore `document.title` (Requirement 32). THE `ShellContext` open view type becomes `"inbox" | "activity" | "members" | "categories" | null`.
**Removal**
8. [NAV-08] THE Frontend SHALL remove `IconRail.tsx`, `TopNav.tsx`, the Home circle, and the Inbox, Dashboard, Categories, and Team items of the V16 lists, and SHALL NOT keep a second navigation component beside the Sidebar, the Top_Menu, and the Nav_Drawer. THE V16 Board_Toolbar "Public link" and "Invite" buttons are removed (Share is in the Top_Menu, SHR-01); THE progress pill, the Board_Switcher, and the "Archived" link stay.
**Layout and shell rules kept**
9. [NAV-09] THE Frontend SHALL lay a page out as SHELL-11 does with the Sidebar in the place of the Icon_Rail: a `--surface-100` canvas with a `--page-x` margin, the Sidebar at the left as a white panel of the `frame` radius role, and one app frame holding the App_Header and the page. THE Sidebar width SHALL be two new tokens, `--sidebar-w` (expanded) and `--sidebar-w-collapsed`, added to `tokens.css` and the Tailwind theme (`w-sidebar`, `w-sidebar-collapsed`); their values are chosen against `01-board-desktop.png` and the 44 pixel hit area, and the Board page keeps its viewport-locked layout (Requirement 14 criterion 18). WHILE the viewport is below 960 pixels, THE frame SHALL fill the screen and there is no Sidebar.
10. [NAV-10] THE App_Header SHALL hold, at 960 pixels and wider: `BrandLogo` (variant `full`, link to `/app/boards`), the Top_Menu (only on a Board), a flexible gap, the bell, and the User_Menu trigger; and below 960 pixels: the menu button, `BrandLogo` (variant `mark`), the Board name on a Board, the bell, and the User_Menu trigger. THE header SHALL show no page context, no search button, and no global search (exclusion 1 of V16). THE Top_Menu SHALL wrap or collapse so that no item is cut off between 960 and 1280 pixels; IF its items do not fit, THEN labels SHALL be hidden and icons with tooltips shown, never a horizontal scroll.
11. [NAV-11] THE Frontend SHALL show no Sidebar, no Top_Menu, no Nav_Drawer, and no Side_Panel on `/app/login`, `/app/register`, `/app/verify`, the signed-out states of `/app/invite/{token}`, `/app/session-expired`, `/app/shared/{token}`, and the Not_Found_Page when reached signed out. THE Shared_View keeps its own light header (SHELL-24). THE first focusable element of every Protected_Route page SHALL be the Skip link, then the App_Header, then the Sidebar, then the main region (SHELL-25).
12. [NAV-12] THE Profile and Log out SHALL stay in the User_Menu only (HDR-02 to HDR-05); the Sidebar and the Nav_Drawer SHALL NOT repeat them.
13. [NAV-13] THE Inbox view SHALL show at the top of its list an "All" and "Unread" switch (a two-option segmented control on the pill track recipe, `role="radiogroup"`, keyboard operable), drawn as `11-inbox-desktop.png`, defaulting to All. "Unread" SHALL load `GET /api/notifications?unread=true`, which THE Backend SHALL add to the existing endpoint: only rows with `read_at` null, the same paging (`limit`, `before`) and order, and `unread_count` unchanged. WHEN a row is marked read while "Unread" is shown, it SHALL stay visible until the next load so the list does not jump. "Mark all as read" keeps its behaviour. THE empty state under "Unread" SHALL read "No unread notifications" (Open Question 70).
