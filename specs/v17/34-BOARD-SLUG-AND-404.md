# Board Slug and 404 Page, V17 (draft)
### Requirement 48: Board Slug and Not Found Page
**User Story:** As a user, I want a Board's address to read like its name, and a clear page when an address is wrong, so that I can share and recognise links and never land on a blank error.
This requirement adds `boards.slug` and the URL `/app/b/{slug}` (Decision BB). Every other endpoint keeps the Board UUID (Requirement 13). Public links and invite links use their own tokens.
#### Acceptance Criteria
**Data and generation**
1. [SLUG-01] THE Backend SHALL add `boards.slug` (text, not null) with a unique index over every Board row, including archived and soft-deleted Boards, in one reversible migration that backfills every existing Board from its name in `created_at` then `id` order (so the earliest Board keeps the plain slug) and drops the column on downgrade.
2. [SLUG-02] THE Backend SHALL generate a slug from the Board name by one pure function `slugify(name)` in `app/common/slug.py`: lower-case the name, split it on white space, remove from each word every character that is not `a` to `z` or `0` to `9`, drop the words that are then empty, and join the rest with `-`. A name that gives an empty slug SHALL use `board`. Examples: "Q3 Launch Plan" gives `q3-launch-plan`; "Design & Dev" gives `design-dev`; "Café Board" gives `caf-board` (Open Question 66); "!!!" gives `board`.
3. [SLUG-03] WHEN the slug is already taken by another Board, THE Backend SHALL append `-2`, `-3`, and so on, using the lowest number from 2 that is free, compared case-sensitively against the stored lower-case slugs. IF two requests create the same slug at once, THEN the unique index SHALL make one fail and the service SHALL retry with the next free suffix (at most 5 times) before it returns HTTP 409.
4. [SLUG-04] WHEN a Board is created, THE Backend SHALL set its slug in the same transaction. WHEN a Board is renamed, THE Backend SHALL regenerate the slug from the new name in the same transaction, ignoring the Board's own row when it checks for a taken slug; a rename that yields the same slug SHALL keep it (no suffix change). THE Backend SHALL NOT keep the old slug (Open Question 46).
5. [SLUG-05] THE Backend SHALL NOT accept a `slug` in any request body or query; the slug is derived and read-only. A request that carries `slug` SHALL receive HTTP 400 `VALIDATION_ERROR` with `details.slug`.
**Endpoint**
6. [SLUG-06] WHEN a signed-in user calls `GET /api/boards/slug/{slug}`, THE Backend SHALL return the Board exactly as `GET /api/boards/{id}` does, with the same role rules and payload, and the same status codes: 401 without a Token, 403 without Board_Access (Open Question 52), and 404 when no non-soft-deleted Board has that slug. THE slug in the path SHALL be matched in lower case and SHALL be rejected with 404 when it contains a character outside `a-z`, `0-9`, and `-`.
7. [SLUG-07] THE Board payloads of `GET /api/boards`, `GET /api/boards/{id}`, `GET /api/boards/slug/{slug}`, and `POST` and `PATCH /api/boards` SHALL carry the read-only `slug`. THE Notification payload SHALL carry `board_slug` beside `board_id` (Requirement 28), and THE `board` object of `GET /api/invite/{token}` SHALL carry `slug`. NO `/api/shared` payload SHALL carry a slug (Requirement 18). THE Backend SHALL regenerate `backend/openapi.json` and `backend/API.md`.
**Frontend**
8. [SLUG-08] THE Board URL SHALL be `/app/b/{slug}`, with the views `/list`, `/calendar`, and `/archived` under it. THE Frontend SHALL load the Board with `GET /api/boards/slug/{slug}` and use the returned `id` for every other call.
9. [SLUG-09] WHEN a Board is renamed in the open page, THE Frontend SHALL replace the URL (not push) with `/app/b/{new slug}`, keep the current view segment and the query string, and refresh the document title (Requirement 32) and the Board_Switcher entry from the response. A second tab on the old slug SHALL show the Not_Found_Page on its next load or request.
10. [SLUG-10] THE public link and the invite link SHALL keep working after a rename, because they carry their own tokens and resolve the Board by it (Requirements 18 and 24).
**Not found page**
11. [SLUG-11] THE Frontend SHALL provide one on-brand Not_Found_Page, `app/not-found.tsx`, used for: any unknown path under `/` and `/app`, any `notFound()` call, an unknown or old Board slug, and an unknown `/app/boards/{id}`. IT SHALL show `BrandLogo`, a heading ("Page not found"), one short message ("The page may have moved, or the link is old."), a decorative illustration made only of Design_System tokens (a large `--accent-tint` numeral "404" or an inline SVG that uses token fills, with no raster file and no hard-coded colour), and two buttons: "Go to boards" (to `/app/boards`) and "Go home" (to `/`). WHEN the visitor is signed out, "Go to boards" SHALL lead to the sign-in page through the guard.
12. [SLUG-12] THE Not_Found_Page SHALL answer HTTP 404, carry `X-Robots-Tag: noindex`, set the document title `RevoBoard | Page not found`, keep one `h1`, expose the buttons as links, work with the keyboard, and fit 360, 414, 960, and 1280 pixels with no horizontal scroll. UNDER `/app` it SHALL show inside the app shell when the visitor is signed in, and bare otherwise.
13. [SLUG-13] THE Not_Found_Page SHALL leak nothing: it SHALL NOT show the requested slug, a Board name, or a hint of whether the Board exists (Open Question 52).
**Rules that stay**
14. [SLUG-14] THE ActivityLog metadata, notifications, logs, and error messages SHALL NOT carry `share_token` or `invite_token` (Requirements 18 and 24); a slug is not a credential and MAY appear in them.
