# Dashboard and My Tasks, V17 (draft)
### Requirement 51: Dashboard
**User Story:** As a Supervisor or Manager, I want a greeting, filters, and a few clear widgets about late, upcoming, and finished work, so that I see what needs attention before I open a Board.
This requirement supersedes DASH-08 and DASH-09 (no greeting, limited widgets) and amends Requirement 43 (DASH-01 to DASH-07 stand, with the path `/app/dashboard`). The existing Overview card and the throughput chart stay. Reference: `design/reference/v2/03-dashboard-desktop.png` and `03-dashboard-mobile.png`; new widgets use the same card recipe. The Dashboard keeps the audience of Requirement 10: a Supervisor sees the Boards it owns, a Manager sees every Board; a Member and an Admin get HTTP 403 (Decision B).
#### Acceptance Criteria
**Greeting and filters**
1. [DASH-10] THE Frontend SHALL show at the top of the Dashboard a greeting "Good morning", "Good afternoon", or "Good evening" followed by the signed-in user's first name (the first white-space-separated word of `full_name`, "there" when empty): morning from 00:00 to 11:59, afternoon from 12:00 to 17:59, evening from 18:00 to 23:59, by the browser's local time. THE greeting SHALL be one pure function, `greeting(date, fullName)`, rendered in a Client Component after mount (the server render shows "Hello"), so there is no hydration mismatch. THE greeting SHALL replace the plain "Dashboard" eyebrow; the document title stays `RevoBoard | Dashboard`.
2. [DASH-11] THE Dashboard SHALL offer three filters in the title block: Board (the existing `BaseCombobox`; a Supervisor sees its owned Boards, a Manager "All boards" and every Board), Member (a `BaseCombobox` of "Anyone" and the people in the selected scope), and Date range (two date fields and presets "Last 7 days", "Last 30 days", "This month"). THE date range SHALL default to the last 30 days (OQ49): `to` is today and `from` is 29 days earlier, in `APP_TIMEZONE`. A change of a filter SHALL refetch once and show the loading state (DASH-07). The filters SHALL be kept in the query string (`?board=&member=&from=&to=`) so a link reproduces the view.
3. [DASH-12] THE filters SHALL apply to the widgets as this table says (Open Question 54). Board and Member apply to every widget. THE date range applies to the widgets that have a date inside the range:
| Widget | Board and Member | Date range applies to |
|---|---|---|
| Overview (existing counts) | yes | no ("Not limited by the date range") |
| Completed per week (existing throughput) | yes | `completed_at` |
| Overdue cards | yes | `end_date` |
| Due tomorrow | yes | no: it has its own "Due on" date filter (default tomorrow), independent of the range (DASH-15) |
| Top 5 priority cards | yes | no ("Not limited by the date range") |
| Finished on time | yes | `completed_at` |
| Finished overdue | yes | `completed_at` |
THE Overview and Top 5 priority widgets, which ignore the range, SHALL show "Not limited by the date range" in `text-xs` `--ink-muted` text. THE Due tomorrow widget has its own filter (DASH-15) and shows no such note.
**Widgets**
4. [DASH-13] THE Dashboard SHALL keep the Overview card and the "Completed per week" chart (DASH-03 and DASH-04) and the Boards table (DASH-05) and Recent activity (DASH-06) as they are, now fed by the filters where the table says so.
5. [DASH-14] THE "Overdue cards" widget SHALL show the count and the first 10 Cards: Open_Cards whose `end_date` is before today (in `APP_TIMEZONE`) and inside the date range, sorted by `end_date` ascending, then priority (high first), then `id`. EACH row shows the title (a link that opens `/app/b/{slug}?card={id}`), the Board name, the priority badge, the assignee name, and the date with the "Overdue" badge. WHEN there are more than 10, THE widget SHALL show "and N more" (no link; the Board's List view is the place to see all).
6. [DASH-15] THE "Due tomorrow" widget (the H-1 list) SHALL have its own date filter, a "Due on" date field in the widget header that defaults to tomorrow and is independent of the global date range (Open Question 54, answered by the owner); it SHALL show the count and the first 10 Open_Cards whose `end_date` equals that date, in the same row layout, sorted by priority (high first), then `id`. WHEN the chosen date is not tomorrow, THE title SHALL read "Due on <date>". THE Board and Member filters still apply; the choice is kept in the query string as `due_on`.
7. [DASH-16] THE "Top 5 priority cards" widget SHALL show up to 5 Open_Cards, sorted by priority (`high`, `medium`, `low`), then the nearest `end_date` (a Card without `end_date` last), then `id`.
8. [DASH-17] THE "Finished on time" and "Finished overdue" widgets SHALL show a count each and, together, a stacked bar with a hidden data table: a Card is finished on time when its `completed_at`, converted to a date in `APP_TIMEZONE`, is on or before its `end_date`, and finished overdue when it is after. A Card without `end_date` SHALL be excluded from both figures and from the bar. ARCHIVED Cards SHALL count (the work was finished, THR-06); soft-deleted Cards SHALL NOT. THE two widgets SHALL show "On time N%" computed from the two counts, and "No finished cards in this period" when both are zero.
9. [DASH-18] THE Dashboard SHALL NOT show estimates, capacity, velocity percentages, or any claim the API does not return (exclusion 6 of V16).
**Endpoint**
10. [DASH-19] THE Backend SHALL provide `GET /api/analytics/dashboard?board=&member=&from=&to=&due_on=` in the `analytics` blueprint (`routes.py`, `service.py`, `repository.py`, `schema.py`), with the role rules of Requirement 10: a Supervisor reads only Boards it owns (a `board` it does not own is 403; no `board` means every Board it owns), a Manager reads any Board (no `board` means every Board), a Member and an Admin receive 403, no Token 401, and an unknown or soft-deleted `board` 404. `member` is a user id and filters Cards by `assignee_id`; an id that is not a UUID is 400. `due_on` is a `YYYY-MM-DD` date for the Due tomorrow widget and defaults to tomorrow in `APP_TIMEZONE` (an invalid date is 400); `from` and `to` are `YYYY-MM-DD`; `from` after `to`, a range longer than 182 days (26 weeks, the throughput limit of THR-07), or an invalid date is 400 `VALIDATION_ERROR` with `details`. WHEN `from` and `to` are absent, THE Backend SHALL use the last 30 days.
11. [DASH-20] THE response SHALL be one pre-aggregated Envelope `data` of: `range` (`from`, `to`), `today` (the date in `APP_TIMEZONE`), `overview` (`total`, `done`, `in_progress`, `overdue`), `throughput` (`weeks` and `total`, as THR-05, for the range, the whole weeks that touch it), `overdue` (`count`, `cards`), `due_tomorrow` (`count`, `cards`), `top_priority` (`cards`), `finished` (`on_time`, `overdue`), and `members` (`id`, `full_name`, and `avatar_url` of the people in scope, no email). A `cards` entry has `id`, `title`, `board_id`, `board_slug`, `board_name`, `priority`, `end_date`, `due_state`, `assignee_name`. NO other Card field and no raw dump of Cards SHALL be returned (Requirement 10 criterion 5).
12. [DASH-21] THE Backend SHALL answer with a number of database queries that does not depend on the number of Cards or Boards (a fixed budget stated in the design: at most 10), using grouped queries and `LIMIT`, and SHALL compute `due_state`, Overdue_Card, and the `completed_at` date with the same functions as the Board views (VIEW-08) and THR-05 (Property 78).
**States**
13. [DASH-22] THE Dashboard SHALL keep the loading, error with Retry, and empty states of DASH-07 for the new widgets; each widget with no rows SHALL show a short empty line ("Nothing overdue", "Nothing due tomorrow", "No cards to rank"); charts follow the chart rules (token colours, an accessible name, a hidden data table). WHILE the viewport is below 960 pixels, THE widgets SHALL stack in one column with no horizontal scroll between 360 and 414 pixels, and the filters SHALL wrap.
### Requirement 52: My Tasks
**User Story:** As a signed-in user, I want one list of the Cards assigned to me across my Boards, grouped by when they are due, so that I know what to do next.
My Tasks has no reference image and is built from the Component_Recipes (Open Question 63).
#### Acceptance Criteria
1. [TASK-01] THE Frontend SHALL provide `/app/my-tasks`, in the Sidebar for a Member, a Supervisor, and a Manager (an Admin does not see the item, and an Admin who opens the path is redirected to `/app/boards`), listing the Cards assigned to the signed-in user on every Board the user can see (Requirement 22), excluding Cards in the Done_Column, archived Cards, soft-deleted Cards, and Cards of a soft-deleted Board. THE document title SHALL be `RevoBoard | My tasks`.
2. [TASK-02] THE page SHALL group the rows in this order: Overdue, Today, Tomorrow, This week, Later, No due date, with a heading and a count per group, and SHALL omit an empty group. THE groups in `APP_TIMEZONE`: Overdue is `end_date` before today; Today is today; Tomorrow is tomorrow; This week is every later day up to and including the Sunday of the current Monday-first week (empty when tomorrow is already in the next week); Later is every day after that; No due date is a null `end_date`. EVERY Card falls in exactly one group (Property 80).
3. [TASK-03] EACH row SHALL show the Card title, the Board name, the priority badge, and the due badge (VIEW-09), and SHALL be a link to `/app/b/{slug}?card={id}`, which opens the Board with the Card modal. WITHIN a group, rows are sorted by `end_date` ascending, then priority (high first), then `id`. THE page SHALL show an empty state "Nothing assigned to you" when no row exists, and loading and error-with-Retry states from the Design_System. WHILE the viewport is below 960 pixels, rows stack with a 44 pixel hit area.
4. [TASK-04] THE Backend SHALL provide `GET /api/me/cards`, for any signed-in user (an Admin gets an empty list, because an Admin cannot be an assignee, Requirement 22), returning one Envelope `data` of `{ "today": "YYYY-MM-DD", "groups": [ { "key", "cards": [...] } ] }` in the order of criterion 2, where a card has `id`, `title`, `board_id`, `board_slug`, `board_name`, `priority`, `end_date`, `due_state`, and `category` (`id`, `name`, `color`) or null. THE Backend SHALL compute the groups from `APP_TIMEZONE`, SHALL use a number of queries that does not depend on the number of Cards, SHALL return no other user's Card, and SHALL return no Email or description.
5. [TASK-05] THE Frontend SHALL read the groups from the API and SHALL NOT group by the browser date.
