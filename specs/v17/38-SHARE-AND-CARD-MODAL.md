# Share Modal and Card Modal, V17 (draft)
### Requirement 55: Share Modal
**User Story:** As a Board_Owner, Manager, or Admin, I want one place to manage how people reach my Board, so that I do not hunt for two buttons and two modals.
This requirement supersedes SHELL-17, PUB-09, and INV-19 to INV-21 for layout only (Decision BH). Behaviour, endpoints, permissions, and visibility rules of Requirements 23, 24, and 31 stay unchanged: the Public_Link is managed by a Manager and an Admin only (PUB-01, PUB-02), the Invite_Link by the Board_Owner, a Manager, and an Admin (INV-02 to INV-05), join requests are decided by a Manager and an Admin only (INV-14 to INV-16). Reference: `design/reference/v2/06-invite-public-link-desktop.png` and `06-invite-public-link-mobile.png`.
#### Acceptance Criteria
1. [SHR-01] THE Frontend SHALL replace the separate "Public link" and "Invite" buttons and modals with one Share item in the Top_Menu (NAV-04) that opens one Share_Modal (`BaseModal`), drawn as `06-invite-public-link-desktop.png` with the Component_Recipes, titled "Share board". THE item SHALL be shown to the Board_Owner, a Manager, and an Admin, and to nobody else. WHERE the viewer decides on requests (a Manager or an Admin) and the Board has pending Join_Requests, THE item SHALL show their count as a teal count badge, capped at "9+".
2. [SHR-02] THE Share_Modal SHALL hold, in this order: (a) the Invite link section: the state, "Create invite link" when off, the full link (the origin plus the `path` of the API, with a copy button, `invite_token` never shown elsewhere), Regenerate, and Disable; (b) the pending Join_Requests list: avatar or initials, full name, username, email, and the time, with Approve and Reject for a Manager and an Admin and no action for the Board_Owner (INV-20, INV-27); (c) the Public link section, with the same controls and read-only wording. EACH section SHALL show only to the roles that may use it: (a) and (b) to the Board_Owner, a Manager, and an Admin; (c) to a Manager and an Admin only (the owner who is neither sees no Public link section and no hint of one).
3. [SHR-03] EVERY create, regenerate, and disable in the Share_Modal SHALL go through ConfirmDialog (FIX-07; the V12 popover forms are replaced). Copy, Approve, and Reject SHALL NOT. A Join_Request decision SHALL keep its V12 behaviour (INV-14, INV-15) and show the result inline.
4. [SHR-04] THE modal SHALL open on `?invite=1` (the Inbox link of a `board_join_requested` notification, Requirement 28) and on the Share item, SHALL keep a copied link out of the document title and the URL, and SHALL send no token to a log. THE Frontend SHALL build the link from `window.location.origin` and the API's `path` (`/app/invite/{token}`, `/app/shared/{token}`, PATH-04). Closing the modal SHALL return focus to the Share item.
5. [SHR-05] THE modal SHALL fit 360 to 414 pixels as a full-width sheet with 44 pixel actions and no horizontal scroll, keep a focus trap and Escape (BaseModal), and show loading and error-with-Retry states for each section independently. THE Shared_View, the invite page, and every API route stay as they are.
### Requirement 56: Card Modal
**User Story:** As a user, I want the Card modal to look like the owner's new design, with the key attributes in one row, so that I can read and edit a Card at a glance.
This requirement rebuilds the layout of the Card modal (Requirement 14 criterion 8, Requirement 25, Requirement 40 criteria for the modal) to match `design/reference/v2/04-card-modal-desktop.png` and `04-card-modal-mobile.png`. Behaviour, permissions, auto-save, and payloads do not change (Decision BH).
#### Acceptance Criteria
1. [CM-01] THE Frontend SHALL draw the Card modal to match `04-card-modal-desktop.png` with the Component_Recipes: the same header, panes, and footer structure as today (a fixed header, two panes that scroll on their own, and a fixed footer) with the reference's spacing, radii, and type, and no value that is not a token. THE footer SHALL hold Archive and Delete (the Delete through ConfirmDialog, FIX-07) and no Save changes or Close button (V12). Below 960 pixels it SHALL follow `04-card-modal-mobile.png` as a full-screen sheet with one scrolling column.
2. [CM-02] THE modal SHALL show under the title one **attribute chip row** with, in this order: status, priority, assignee, dates, and category. THE status chip SHALL show the Card's Column name (a `BaseBadge` with the Column's dot colour) and SHALL be read-only (Open Question 58, exclusion 5); a Card changes Column only by drag-and-drop. THE priority, assignee, dates, and category chips SHALL keep their popovers, their one-field auto-save (Requirement 25), and their permissions (Requirement 4); the assignee popover SHALL list only active and available users (ADM-08); the dates chip shows the due badge (VIEW-09).
3. [CM-03] THE modal SHALL keep the cards of the left and right panes (Details, Attachments, Subtasks; Comments, Activity), the comment rules (Requirement 7), the Subtask rules (Requirement 27), the attachment rules (the file and link behaviour), and the rule that comments and activity render only when the API sends them. IT SHALL add nothing the reference draws that has no spec basis (exclusions of V15 and V16: estimate, `status` dropdown, `@mention`, emoji, bookmark, share and Done buttons, a second assignee).
4. [CM-04] THE modal SHALL open from `/app/b/{slug}?card={id}` and from the List, Calendar, and Archived views, SHALL set and restore `document.title` (Requirement 32), SHALL show an archived Card read-only with a Restore action (VIEW-07) when opened from the Archived view, and SHALL show the not-available message of Requirement 28 when the Card is archived (outside the Archived view), deleted, or inaccessible.
5. [CM-05] THE Frontend SHALL keep the fixed-size rules (every pane scrolls on its own, `lg:grid-rows-1`), the `aria-live="polite"` field status, the focus trap, Escape to close, and the "closing waits for a save in flight" rule of Requirement 25. THE existing tests of the Card modal SHALL be updated, not deleted (task 39.12 rule).
