# UI Fixes, V17 (draft)
### Requirement 45: UI Fixes
**User Story:** As a user, I want the sign-in pages, forms, selects, and confirmations to behave consistently, so that the product feels finished and I never lose work to an accidental click.
This requirement fixes seven defects the owner listed and adds the one shared confirmation dialog (Decision AY). It changes no data and no endpoint. Every visual value comes from Design_System tokens (Requirement 38); the references are `design/reference/v2/05-auth-desktop.png`, `05-auth-mobile.png`, `01-board-desktop.png`, and `13-activity-desktop.png`.
#### Acceptance Criteria
**Sign-in and register pages**
1. [FIX-01] THE Frontend SHALL draw `BrandLogo` on the login and register pages with a transparent background: no tile, fill, or border behind it on the brand panel or on the form side. THE logo files in `public/brand/` SHALL carry an alpha channel; IF the supplied PNG inside either SVG has an opaque background, THEN the implementer SHALL ask the owner for a transparent file and SHALL NOT paint over the image (Open Question 21, V11). A test SHALL read the corner pixels of the embedded PNG and assert that they are fully transparent.
2. [FIX-02] THE login and register pages SHALL use the same canvas and surface tokens as the Boards page: the page background is `--surface-100`, and the form card uses the white surface, radius role, and shadow token of the app frame (SHELL-11), with no second colour or radius of their own. THE brand panel keeps `--gradient-brand` (DS-06). A test SHALL assert that the three class sets resolve to the same token names on the auth layout and on the app shell.
3. [FIX-03] WHEN the user edits a field, THE Frontend SHALL clear that field's error at once, both a client validation error and an error from `error.details` of a previous submit; the error SHALL NOT come back until the next blur or submit finds the new value invalid. Clearing one field SHALL NOT clear another field's error.
4. [FIX-04] WHEN the user submits the login or register form, THE Frontend SHALL validate every field before it sends a request, SHALL show the error of every invalid field at once (not only the first), SHALL move focus to the first invalid field, and SHALL send no request while any field is invalid. EACH invalid field SHALL have `aria-invalid="true"` and its error linked through `aria-describedby`. WHEN the Backend answers `400 VALIDATION_ERROR`, THE Frontend SHALL show every key of `error.details` beside its field in the same pass.
**Selects, comboboxes, and dropdowns**
5. [FIX-05] IN every select, combobox, and dropdown menu (`BaseCombobox`, `BaseMenu`, and every `components/ui` primitive behind them), THE Frontend SHALL show an option's hover highlight while the pointer is over it and SHALL remove it when the pointer leaves, so that at most one option shows a highlight from the pointer at a time and none shows it after the pointer left the list. THE keyboard highlight (arrow keys, Home, End, type-ahead) SHALL keep working and SHALL move the highlight independently of the pointer; WHEN the pointer then moves over another option, the highlight SHALL follow the pointer. A test SHALL fire `pointermove` and `pointerleave` on options and assert the highlighted attribute each time.
**Team panel**
6. [FIX-06] THE Members view of the Side_Panel (the Team panel of V16) SHALL leave `--space-4` between its header and the member search input. IN the build of Phase 1 the view is still called Team_View; Requirement 47 renames it.
**Confirmation**
7. [FIX-07] THE Frontend SHALL provide one `ConfirmDialog` (`components/shared/ConfirmDialog.tsx`), built on `BaseModal`, and SHALL open it before these actions run: (a) create: a Board, a Column, a Category, a Card, a user (Admin), an Invite_Link, and a Public_Link; (b) delete, remove, disable, or regenerate: a Board (soft delete), a Column, a Category, a Card, a Subtask, a comment, a reply, an attachment, a Board member, an Invite_Link, a Public_Link, and a profile picture; (c) the toggles and changes named in Requirement 53 criterion 8. THE comment, reply, Subtask, attachment, and Join_Request creates are not in the list (Open Question 51).
8. [FIX-08] THE dialog SHALL name the item and the action in its title and body ("Delete column “Review”?", "Create board “Q3 plan”?"), SHALL have a primary action whose label repeats the verb ("Delete", "Create") and a Cancel action, SHALL use the `danger` button recipe for a delete and the primary recipe for a create, SHALL close on Escape, on a scrim click, and on Cancel without running the action, and SHALL return focus to the control that opened it. THE primary action SHALL show a busy state while the request runs and SHALL keep the dialog open with the error message when the request fails.
9. [FIX-09] THE Frontend SHALL trap focus inside the dialog while it is open, move initial focus to Cancel for a delete and to the primary action for a create, and SHALL announce the title through `aria-labelledby` and the body through `aria-describedby`. WHILE the viewport is below 960 pixels, THE dialog SHALL fit between 360 and 414 pixels with its two actions on one row or stacked, never cut off.
10. [FIX-10] THE Frontend SHALL replace every use of `BaseConfirm` (the `popover` and `modal` variants of V12) with `ConfirmDialog` and SHALL remove `BaseConfirm` once none is left. THE `structure.md` list of shared primitives SHALL name `ConfirmDialog` in place of `BaseConfirm` (task 39.51).
11. [FIX-11] THE Frontend SHALL keep the existing behaviour of each confirmed action (endpoints, permissions, activity rows, notifications); only the confirmation step changes. A test SHALL, for each action of FIX-07, assert that no request is sent before the dialog is confirmed and exactly one is sent after.
