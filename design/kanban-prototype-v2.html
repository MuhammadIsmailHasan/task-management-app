Frequently Asked Questions

What columns are supported in RevoTask boards? expand_more

RevoTask features a standard 5-stage agile pipeline: Todo, In Progress, Review, Revision, and Done. You can seamlessly drag and drop tasks across columns with HTML5 native drag controls.

How do private boards and join requests work? expand_more

All newly created boards are private to their creator. Owners can create Public Share Links. When teammates click the link, they submit a Join Request that board admins can approve or decline directly from the board settings.

How does automatic task progress calculation work? expand_more

Each task card can contain nested checklist subtasks. As team members mark items complete, RevoTask immediately calculates and visualizes the percentage progress on both the card and the column summary.

What team roles are available? expand_more

RevoTask supports four role levels: Owner (full board configuration and member management), Admin (board modifications and request management), Member (task creation, editing, and checklist updates), and Viewer (read-only inspection).
